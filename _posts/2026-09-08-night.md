### Firefighters' strike: what does the draft civil protection law presented to unions contain?


{% include figure.html
    src="https://www.ici.fr/pikapi/images/96017238-66e1-4c7d-96aa-6d268876aa7b/920x517?webp=false"
    alt="Firefighters' strike: what does the draft civil protection law presented to unions contain?"
    caption="Firefighters' strike: what does the draft civil protection law presented to unions contain?" %}


The draft law on modernizing civil protection, presented to firefighters' unions this Tuesday, aims to improve emergency response organization and strengthen risk prevention. However, the unions criticized the draft, arguing that it neglects the issue of resources necessary to ensure optimal effectiveness in emergency response.

The draft law proposes several measures to improve emergency response organization, including the creation of a national emergency service and the strengthening of coordination between local emergency services. It also includes measures to strengthen risk prevention, such as improving ongoing firefighter training and developing computer tools to better identify high-risk areas.

However, the unions emphasized that it is necessary to provide the necessary financial and material resources to enable firefighters to carry out their missions. They also criticized the government's lack of commitment to the working conditions of firefighters, particularly in terms of salaries and working hours.

In conclusion, the draft law on modernizing civil protection aims to improve emergency response organization and strengthen risk prevention, but the unions have emphasized that it is necessary to provide the necessary resources to ensure optimal effectiveness in emergency response.
