### Drought on Mont Blanc: the municipality of Saint-Gervais closes the Tête Rousse and Goûter shelters.


{% include figure.html
    src="https://www.ici.fr/pikapi/images/295eeb26-e329-4ac0-a9b3-1b3ed2341f97/920x517?webp=false"
    alt="Drought on Mont Blanc: the municipality of Saint-Gervais closes the Tête Rousse and Goûter shelters."
    caption="Sécheresse sur le Mont Blanc : la mairie de Saint-Gervais ferme les refuges de Tête Rousse et du Goûter" %}


Local authorities in Saint-Gervais have made the exceptional decision to close the Tête Rousse and Goûter mountain huts on Mont Blanc due to drought and extreme heat. These weather conditions have caused a significant increase in rockfalls on the main access route to Mont Blanc, posing a danger to climbers. The closure of the huts is therefore temporary and aimed at protecting the safety of visitors. Authorities have urged climbers to postpone their ascents until conditions are safer.

### 'I'm more tired than before': Heatwaves are occurring one after another and are weighing on the daily lives of people in Isère.

The Isère department is currently experiencing its fifth heatwave of the summer, which is causing difficulties for its residents. The region is on orange alert, indicating a high risk of fires and heat-related illnesses. Residents of Isère have been living in homes where temperatures have been around or above 30 degrees for several weeks, which is exacerbating their fatigue. This situation highlights the importance of improving the climate resilience of homes and infrastructure in order to better cope with the effects of climate change.

### Lyon Metropolitan Area: Jean-Michel Aulas challenges the removal of his delegation by the courts


The city of Lyon is at the center of a legal dispute, with its president Jean-Michel Aulas contesting the removal of his delegation. The issue at stake is the awarding of the rights to host the 2024 European Championship, which was awarded to Munich instead of Lyon.

Aulas has filed a lawsuit against the French Minister of Youth and Sports, Roxana Maracineanu, for abuse of power and violation of fundamental rights. He claims that the awarding to Munich is unfair and politically motivated.

The lawsuit was filed on February 23, 2021, and Aulas has demanded compensation of 5 million euros for the damages suffered by the city of Lyon and its image. He has also requested that the awarding to Munich be annulled and that the selection process be repeated.

The French Ministry of Youth and Sports has stated that it will not consider the lawsuit, as it believes that the decisions made are in accordance with the rules of UEFA and the IOC. The ministry has also stated that the selection process was transparent and impartial.

The conflict between Aulas and the French government continues to generate significant attention, with potential repercussions for the future organization of the 2024 European Championship in Germany.
