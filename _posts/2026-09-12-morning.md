The declassified documents were published by the National Security Archive, an independent research center based at George Washington University. The center's director, Thomas Blanton, said that the documents provide a more complete picture of the CIA's knowledge of Al-Qaeda before the attacks.

The declassified documents are a significant contribution to the history of the September 11 attacks and to the understanding of the fight against international terrorism. They provide a better understanding of Al-Qaeda's activities before the attacks, as well as the CIA's efforts to track and counter this terrorist group.

### British MPs reject assisted dying again, the bill buried
