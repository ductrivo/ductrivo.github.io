### Drôme. Whether EPR (European Protected Area) or not, the Tricastin basin will focus on health and mobility.


{% include figure.html
    src="https://cdn-s-www.ledauphine.com/images/B68DB7B7-82A6-44D6-9ED5-632954F3665E/NW_listB/epr-ou-pas-le-bassin-du-tricastin-mettra-l-accent-sur-la-sante-et-les-mobilites-1792173358.jpg"
    alt="Drôme. Whether EPR (European Protected Area) or not, the Tricastin basin will focus on health and mobility."
    caption="Drôme. Whether EPR (European Protected Area) or not, the Tricastin basin will focus on health and mobility." %}


The Tricastin basin, located in the Drôme department, will focus on health and mobility, according to a meeting of Drôme Sud Provence (CCDSP) elected officials held in Donzère on October 14. The goal is to define priorities for action in terms of housing, mobility, services, and economic development.

Marie Fernandez, president of the interco, recalled the origins of the territorial project. Although the initial project included the construction of an EPR nuclear power plant in Graveyron, the elected officials decided to focus on more social and environmental projects.

Priorities for action could include improving road and rail infrastructure, promoting sustainable tourism, developing industrial and commercial zones, and establishing social and medical services.

The elected officials also discussed the need for close cooperation with local authorities to implement these projects. They emphasized the importance of ensuring sustainable and equitable growth in the Tricastin basin.

Finally, they also addressed the issue of managing radioactive waste from the Cruas nuclear power plant, which has been in operation since 1972. They emphasized the importance of complying with environmental and health standards to protect public health and the natural environment.
