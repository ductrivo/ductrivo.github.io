### Drought: 'almost non-existent' rainfall since early September, and the soil has never been this dry at this time of year.


{% include figure.html
    src="https://www.ici.fr/pikapi/images/374c69ec-42af-44dd-9976-9ba3f4ab145e/920x517?webp=false"
    alt="Drought: 'almost non-existent' rainfall since early September, and the soil has never been this dry at this time of year."
    caption="Drought: 'almost non-existent' rainfall since early September, and the soil has never been this dry at this time of year." %}


Drought in France: According to Météo-France, there has been almost no rain since the beginning of September. Since the beginning of October, France has only experienced one day of significant rainfall. As a result, the soil is exceptionally dry at this time of year, after a summer characterized by a lack of precipitation. With only 8.2 mm of rain falling between September 1st and 20th, soil moisture levels are the lowest in several years. This drought could have negative consequences for agriculture, forestry, and the quality of drinking water. Authorities have called for increased vigilance regarding forest fires.
