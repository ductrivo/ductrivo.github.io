### From a simple miner to a discoverer of dolomite, discover the portrait of Déodat Gratet de Dolomieu


Déodat Gratet de Dolomieu is a remarkable figure in the history of Isère, born in 1750. He was successively a Templar, a duellist, a governor, and a geologist.

In his youth, he joined the Templar order before the suppression of the order forced him to leave his monastery in 1798. He then devoted himself to politics and became governor of the department of Isère.

His interest in geology began when he was appointed professor of mineralogy at the Academy of Sciences in 1795. He made several trips to Europe to study rocks and minerals, notably in Italy, where he discovered dolomite in 1791.

He was also an active duellist, participating in over 30 duels during his life. He died in 1801 as a result of being killed in a duel against the Marquis de La Valette.

Therefore, Déodat Gratet de Dolomieu is known for his contributions to geology, particularly his discovery of dolomite, and for his turbulent life, which combines politics, religion, and duels.

The restaurant opened in 2019 and quickly gained a solid reputation in the region. With their entry into Gault & Millau, Claire and Samuel hope to continue attracting food lovers and nature enthusiasts to their unique establishment.
