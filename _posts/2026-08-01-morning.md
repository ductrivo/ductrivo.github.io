### Electric fishing in Isère: emergency rescue operation for fish in the Méaudret River


{% include figure.html
    src="https://www.ici.fr/pikapi/images/454f6b73-d057-4798-9997-8c2cd141e411/920x517?webp=false"
    alt="Electric fishing in Isère: emergency rescue operation for fish in the Méaudret River"
    caption="Pêche électrique en Isère : opération sauvetage d'urgence de poissons dans la rivière du Méaudret" %}


An emergency fish rescue operation was launched in the Méaudret River, in Isère, by the Isère Fishing Federation on Friday, August 31st. This operation involved electrofishing to rescue fish threatened by the river's drought. The affected area was located on the Vercors plateau. The goal was to save as many fish as possible, which were threatened by the drop in water levels due to the drought. The teams used an electrofishing technique to catch the fish and relocate them to wetter areas. The operation allowed for the rescue of a large number of fish, but it is still necessary to monitor the situation, as the drought continues to be a problem in the region.

    alt="Drôme. Fire risks: the prefecture prohibits the use of ball traps in the department until August 10th."
    caption="Drôme. Risques d'incendie : la préfecture interdit les ball-traps dans le département jusqu’au 10 août" %}


The Drôme department has imposed a temporary ban on the use of ball traps until August 10 due to the fire risk. The prefecture made this decision because this sport involves shooting with a shotgun at moving targets or clay targets, which can easily cause a fire in the current dry conditions. Participants must therefore comply with this measure to ensure public safety and prevent any fire risk.
