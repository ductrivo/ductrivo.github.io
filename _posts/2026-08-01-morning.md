### Livret A, electricity, gas prices: what changes from August 1, 2026

    src="https://www.ici.fr/pikapi/images/17ffdecb-47a1-4157-acef-92ac0f748a96/920x517?webp=false"

    caption="Livret A, électricité, prix du gaz : ce qui change à partir du 1er août 2026" %}


Starting August 1, 2026, several changes will come into effect in France:

1. Increase in the interest rate on the Livret A, a popular savings account.
2. Increase in the price of electricity for individuals.
3. Decrease in the price of natural gas for households.
4. End of aggressive telephone sales, a commercial practice considered inappropriate and intrusive.
5. School return allowance, a financial aid intended for families to help cover the costs of children's school equipment.

These changes may have an impact on the personal finances and energy consumption of French households. It is therefore important to stay informed about the details of these changes in order to take appropriate measures accordingly.

### 'Convincing fake satellite images': a new AI tool removed from Google Earth to address the risks of disinformation.


Google Earth's satellite imagery service has removed an artificial intelligence (AI) tool called "Deepfake Satellite Images Generator" due to the risk of spreading misinformation. This software allowed the creation of convincing but falsified satellite images, which could lead to the spread of disinformation on the platform.

Google made this decision in response to growing concerns about the use of technologies such as AI to create false information and manipulate data. False satellite images can have serious consequences, including in national security, natural resource management, and copyright.

Google has also announced that it will implement measures to detect and block false satellite images on its platform. These measures include the use of content analysis technologies, collaboration with experts in geography and AI researchers to identify and eliminate false information.

Google's decision is a response to a growing problem in the digital world: the use of advanced technologies to create false information and manipulate data, which can have serious consequences for society. Businesses and governments must take steps to protect their integrity and prevent the spread of disinformation.

*[Source](https://www.leparisien.fr/high-tech/des-fausses-images-satellites-convaincantes-un-nouvel-outil-dia-retire-de-google-earth-face-aux-risques-de-desinformation-31-07-2026-7QAZJY5PQNFURC4DMJ5K65YRNY.php)*
