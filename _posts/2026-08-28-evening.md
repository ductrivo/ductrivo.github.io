### Thunderstorms in Val-de-Marne: Why was the department not placed on red alert?


The Val-de-Marne department experienced violent storms on Tuesday, June 15, causing significant flooding and damage. Despite the extreme weather conditions, the department was not placed on red alert by Météo-France.

The main reason is that a red alert can only be issued at the national level, not at the regional level. In fact, for a red alert to be issued, the weather conditions must be extreme across the entire French territory or in a large part of it.

The Val-de-Marne was placed on orange alert, which indicates a significant but not extreme risk of heavy rain and storms. This alert was lifted the following morning.

Local authorities stated that the flooding was the worst since 2016, with estimated damage of several million euros. Rescue teams were deployed to help residents affected by the storms.

The Val-de-Marne was not placed on red alert because the extreme weather conditions were localized and did not affect the entire French territory. The orange alert allowed residents to be warned and to organize measures to protect against storms.

### 'We are no longer producing anything in France': the last day at the Fibre Excellence paper pulp factory in Tarascon


The permanent closure of the Fibre Excellence paper pulp mill in Tarascon, in the Gard region, marks the disappearance of the last French paper pulp production plant. This decision results in the loss of approximately 100 direct and indirect jobs, as well as the permanent cessation of paper pulp production in France.

The mill, which was created in 2008 by the Spanish group Ence, produced approximately 150,000 tonnes of paper pulp annually, primarily for cardboard and paper mills.

The closure of the mill is due to a combination of factors, including international competition, high production costs in France, and a lack of investment in the paper industry in France.

This decision has been criticized by unions, who have pointed out that the closure of the mill would result in a significant loss for the local and national economy, as well as an increase in paper pulp imports.

The permanent closure of the Fibre Excellence mill in Tarascon marks the end of a chapter in the history of the paper industry in France and highlights the challenges that this industry must face to survive in the international market.
