### Where does the name of the Serre-Cocu viewpoint in the northwest of the Vercors come from?


{% include figure.html
    src="https://www.ici.fr/pikapi/images/f57bc9b9-e845-4c80-b124-09f3fa398938/920x517?webp=false"
    alt="Where does the name of the Serre-Cocu viewpoint in the northwest of the Vercors come from?"
    caption="Where does the name of the Serre-Cocu viewpoint in the northwest of the Vercors come from?" %}


The Serre-Cocu viewpoint is located in the northwest of the Vercors region in France. Its name comes from a curious designation that is not always associated with what one might expect.

The word "Serre" is a geographical term referring to a hill or ridge. The word "Cocu", on the other hand, has been interpreted in different ways over the years. A popular theory suggests that this name comes from the Provençal word "cogu", which means "rooster" in French, in reference to the rocks that evoke the silhouette of a rooster that can be found on the site.

Another theory suggests that the name could come from the Provençal expression "cocu de merle", which refers to a bird, the blackbird, which is known for being aggressive towards other birds. This theory is based on the presence of a large number of blackbirds in the Vercors region.

Finally, there is a third theory according to which the name could be linked to past human activity in the region. Indeed, "Serre-Cocu" could be a derivative of "sarra cocu", which means in Occitan "enclosed field" or "cultivated land".

Although these theories are different, they are all based on interpretations of the Provençal dialect and the local geography. Today, the Serre-Cocu viewpoint is a popular tourist site for its panoramic view of the surrounding area.
