### Drôme. What the fountains tell us about the history of the village in Dieulefit


{% include figure.html
    src="https://cdn-s-www.ledauphine.com/images/19C0734D-E059-4640-8937-D69A0B28534B/NW_listB/ce-que-les-fontaines-racontent-de-l-histoire-du-village-a-dieulefit-1787421149.jpg"
    alt="Drôme. What the fountains tell us about the history of the village in Dieulefit"
    caption="Drôme. What the fountains tell us about the history of the village in Dieulefit" %}


The village of Dieulefit, located in the Drôme department, has a rich and varied history thanks to its fountains. One of them, the Terron fountain, is particularly noteworthy. It is located near the mysterious "touvières" of Dieulefit, natural springs that played an important role in the local history.

Aline Raffy, a guide and lecturer at the Dieulefit-Bourdeaux tourism office, offers guided tours to discover this exceptional heritage. She tells the story of the fountain and the touvières, as well as their historical and cultural importance for the village.

The salt mines of Dieulefit were operated from antiquity until the 19th century to extract salt. The Terron fountain was built in the 17th century to supply water for the salt works. The touvières, which are natural sources of underground water, also played an important role in the extraction of salt.

Aline Raffy explains that the Terron fountain is a remarkable architectural work, with its stone columns and terracotta decoration. She also tells the legends and superstitions that surround the touvières, some of which date back to antiquity.

The guided tours offered by Aline Raffy allow visitors to discover the cultural and historical heritage of Dieulefit in depth. They are a unique opportunity to learn more about the region, its history and traditions.
