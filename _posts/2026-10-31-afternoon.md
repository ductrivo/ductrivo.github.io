### [1/2] Jacques Métais case: the 'mummy of the Calanques'


The Jacques Métais case, also known as "the mummy of the Calanques," is a French criminal case that occurred in 1976. Jacques Métais, a 42-year-old man, was found dead in the Calanques of Marseille. His body was mutilated and preserved with salt and herbs, giving the impression of a mummy.

The investigation revealed that Métais had disappeared on August 28, 1976, after leaving a bar in Marseille with two unknown men. His body was found on September 3 in the Calanques, near the village of Cassis.

The autopsy revealed that Métais had been subjected to a violent attack and had been killed with an axe. Investigators suspected a crime of passion or a murder for the purpose of robbery, but the case remains unsolved to this day.

The death of Jacques Métais attracted a great deal of media attention and inspired several books and films. The place where his body was found has become a popular tourist site, known as "the mummy's cave."
