"Égrégore" is scheduled to be released on October 1, 2021. The album will be available in CD, vinyl, and digital download formats. A deluxe edition of the album will include bonus tracks and a photo booklet.

Mylène Farmer has already announced a series of concerts to promote the album, which will take place in Paris in December 2021.
