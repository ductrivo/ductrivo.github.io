In France, mayors have limited power to cancel concerts on their territory without the agreement of the promoter or the artist. However, they can take certain measures to limit noise or public disturbances. For example, they can impose specific conditions in the permit to organize an event, such as concert end times or limits on the number of spectators.

In the event of antisocial or illegal behavior by the artist, mayors can take disciplinary measures under the General Code of Local Authorities (CGCT). For example, they can temporarily suspend the permit to organize an event for a specified period.

Finally, mayors can also ask the police to monitor the event and intervene if necessary in the event of public disturbances. They can also collaborate with non-governmental organizations (NGOs) that work against sexual violence to provide assistance to victims and prevent attacks.

In conclusion, mayors have a few options for canceling a concert or limiting its impact, but their power is limited by the General Code of Local Authorities and the need to obtain the agreement of the promoter or the artist.
