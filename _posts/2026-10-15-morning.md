### Handball: PSG qualifies for the main round of the Champions League


Paris Saint-Germain (PSG) has successfully qualified for the main round of the Men's Handball Champions League, after defeating the Spanish club FC Barcelona in the group stage semi-finals. The victory was achieved with a score of 28-27, which allowed PSG to top its group with 14 points, ahead of FC Barcelona (10 points) and Aalborg Håndbold (6 points).

PSG began its European campaign by defeating the Hungarian club Veszprém KSE with a score of 32-28, followed by a victory against the Danish club Aalborg Håndbold (31-27). FC Barcelona was defeated twice by PSG, in the away and home matches.

The main round of the Champions League consists of eight teams, including the top four from each group of the group stage, as well as the two best second-placed teams. PSG finished first in its group and was therefore directly qualified for the main round, without having to play the playoffs.

The main round will take place from February 12 to 14, 2023, with home and away matches between each team. The top two in each group will qualify for the semi-finals, which will be played on May 5 and 6, 2023. The winner of the Champions League will be determined in the final, scheduled for May 13, 2023.

PSG has already won the Champions League in 2017 and is currently one of the favorites to win the title this year.
