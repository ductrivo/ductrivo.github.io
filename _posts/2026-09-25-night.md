### 'Companies have responsibilities': The UN now lists 214 companies linked to Israeli settlements.


The United Nations recently identified 214 companies linked to Israeli settlements. These companies are required to respect human rights and international humanitarian law standards, particularly in the context of the Israeli-Palestinian conflict.
