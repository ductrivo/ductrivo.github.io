### 'Ensuring freedom of navigation in the Strait of Hormuz': France seeks to propose its resolution to the UN Security Council


France proposes a resolution to the UN to ensure freedom of navigation in the Strait of Hormuz, which is a crucial passage for global maritime traffic. This initiative aims to ensure regional security and stability by addressing potential threats to the freedom of navigation in this strategic area. France calls on members of the Security Council to take measures to prevent any arbitrary ban or restriction on navigation in the Strait of Hormuz, in accordance with international law and the principles of international freedom of navigation. This initiative is a response to recent actions by Iran, which has threatened to close the strait if the United States do not lift their economic sanctions against the country. France hopes that this resolution will be approved by members of the Security Council and that it will contribute to maintaining peace and stability in the region.
