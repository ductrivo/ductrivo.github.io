### Glass or plastic, exposure, surface: everything you need to know about setting up a greenhouse


{% include figure.html
    src="https://www.ici.fr/pikapi/images/4edd5116-f496-4500-91d2-f3bb7bbcb29d/920x517?webp=false"
    alt="Glass or plastic, exposure, surface: everything you need to know about setting up a greenhouse"
    caption="Glass or plastic, exposure, surface: everything you need to know about setting up a greenhouse" %}


Installing a greenhouse: essential knowledge

Autumn is the ideal time to install a greenhouse to protect certain plants from the cold, continue gardening, and prepare for spring crops. Jean-Yves Meignen, in his gardening column, emphasizes the importance of carefully considering its use, location, and materials before starting.

The greenhouse can be made of glass or plastic. The choice will depend on the budget, the desired size, and local climate conditions. Glass greenhouses are more expensive but more durable, while plastic greenhouses are less expensive but less resistant to the cold.

The location is also crucial for the success of the greenhouse. It should be south-facing or west-facing to receive maximum sunlight. It is also important to consider nearby trees and buildings that may obstruct the light.

Finally, it is important to carefully choose the materials used for building the greenhouse. Materials resistant to moisture are preferable to avoid condensation and mildew problems. It is also important to check that the materials are suitable for local climate conditions, especially in the event of heavy frosts or heavy rain.

In conclusion, installing a greenhouse can be a worthwhile investment for both amateur and professional gardeners. It is important to carefully consider its use, location, and materials before embarking on this venture.
