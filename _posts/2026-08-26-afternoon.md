The start of the school or work year can be a source of stress and negative emotions for some, and this phenomenon is known as "back-to-school blues." Here are four tips for dealing with it:

1. Plan: Anticipating tasks and upcoming events can reduce anxiety related to the start of the school year.
2. Take care of yourself: Maintaining a healthy lifestyle, including proper nutrition, regular exercise, and rest, is important for maintaining a good mood.
3. Connect with others: Talking to friends or colleagues can help you feel less isolated and get back on track.
4. Give yourself time: Not putting too much pressure on yourself is crucial to avoid fatigue and depression. It is important to take time for yourself, have fun, and relax.

By following these tips, it is possible to reduce the back-to-school blues and have a more enjoyable start to the year.
