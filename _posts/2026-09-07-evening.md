### Fake bank advisor, fraudulent SMS: manipulation scams are on the rise in 2025.


{% include figure.html
    src="https://www.ici.fr/pikapi/images/92314915-d5c0-46d3-9951-6274958dbe46/920x517?webp=false"
    alt="Fake bank advisor, fraudulent SMS: manipulation scams are on the rise in 2025."
    caption="Fake bank advisor, fraudulent SMS: manipulation scams are on the rise in 2025." %}


In 2025, scams involving manipulation saw a significant increase, according to the report published on Monday by the Payment Security Observatory (OSMP) of the Bank of France. The amounts related to these scams, including those involving fake bank advisors, increased by 34% in one year. This trend is due to the increasing use of digital technologies to commit these crimes, as well as the growing vulnerability of users to new scam methods. Victims are often chosen from the most vulnerable and least informed individuals regarding the risks associated with these scams. The OSMP has called on individuals and businesses to remain vigilant against new scam methods and to take security measures to protect their personal data.

Claudie Gallay accurately describes the harshness of the climate and conditions, as well as the courage and determination of the sled dog drivers who undertook this journey.
