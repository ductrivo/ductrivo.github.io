### 'The solutions are known but not widely implemented': cities particularly affected by climate change


Cities, which represent a large portion of the world's population, are particularly vulnerable to the effects of climate change. While solutions are known to reduce these impacts, their implementation remains limited.

Cities are responsible for a high consumption of energy and greenhouse gases due to their density and reliance on individual transportation. Floods, storms, extreme heat, and air pollution are examples of the effects of climate change that affect cities.

Solutions to reduce these impacts include transitioning to a low-carbon economy, improving energy efficiency, and developing sustainable transportation. Cities can also use technologies such as solar panels, energy storage batteries, and smart grids to reduce their carbon footprint.

Local initiatives are important for implementing these solutions. Cities can collaborate with non-governmental organizations, businesses, and local governments to develop climate action plans. Funding programs such as the Green Climate Fund can also help cities implement these solutions.

Finally, it is important to emphasize that cities play a crucial role in combating climate change. By reducing their carbon footprint and adapting to the effects of climate change, cities can contribute to a better quality of life for their residents and a more sustainable planet.
