### 'The German people within a single state': An AFD official wants to reclaim Alsace and Lorraine.


The right-wing German party, known as the AFD, has seen one of its officials express a desire to reintegrate Alsace and Lorraine into Germany. This territory, currently French, was annexed by Germany after the Franco-Prussian War in 1871 before being returned to France after World War I.
