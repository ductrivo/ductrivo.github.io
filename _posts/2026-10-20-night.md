The most affected regions are:
- The Limousin region with 120,000 wild boars killed, representing almost 12% of the total national figure.
- The Lozère region with 95,000 wild boars, representing approximately 9.5%.
- The Cantal region with 87,000 wild boars, representing almost 9%.

### Luxembourg life insurance: Why this popular investment among French people is threatened by the government?


Luxembourg life insurance: Why this popular investment for French people is threatened by the government.

Luxembourg has long been an attractive place for French people who want to invest in life insurance due to its tax advantages. However, the French government has taken measures to reduce this practice, which is considered to be avoiding income tax.

Specifically, since 2017, French people who invest in foreign life insurance must pay an additional 30% tax on the profits they obtain from these investments. Furthermore, in 2019, the French government announced that it would end the tax advantages for French people who invest in Luxembourg life insurance, starting January 1, 2020.

These measures have had a significant impact on the life insurance industry in Luxembourg, which has seen a significant decline in French investment in these products since their implementation. Luxembourg insurers have also been affected by the economic crisis related to the Covid-19 pandemic, which has led to a decline in investment in life insurance in general.

Despite these challenges, Luxembourg insurers continue to offer attractive products to French people, particularly thanks to their expertise and remaining tax advantages. However, the situation remains uncertain, and it is possible that the French government will take further measures to reduce the attractiveness of Luxembourg life insurance for French people.
