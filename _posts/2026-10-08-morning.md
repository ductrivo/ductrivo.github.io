The police were called in to maintain order and prevent riots. The high school students mobilized against a reform of the school assessment system, which provides for more frequent and rigorous exams. They have also expressed their opposition to the increase in school fees. Authorities have called on the high school students to respect public order and not to use violence to express their opinions.

### 'We must show respect to the leader of another country': Tensions rise between Japan and China


Relations between Japan and China have recently become strained, with an increase in tensions. This situation is due to a call for solidarity with Taiwan, led by Japanese Prime Minister Fumio Kishida, which has triggered a negative reaction from Chinese authorities. The Chinese have considered the call a violation of the principle of a neutral foreign policy towards Taiwan and have demanded formal apologies from the Japanese government. Japan has responded that it respects the established diplomatic relations between China and Taiwan, but has insisted that its support for democracy and human rights is universal. The two countries also have differences on historical issues, particularly those related to World War II, which have contributed to further tensions between them. It is important to note that these tensions could have negative repercussions on the economy and trade relations between Japan and China, two of Asia's major economies.

### 'Oil leaks,' 'short circuits': after the fires, an audit raises concerns about the maintenance of buses in the Compiègne area.


Audit reveals oil leaks and short circuits on Compiegne buses, leading to accusations against them in the fires that occurred. The audit was conducted by the Picardie Regional Environment, Development and Housing Department (DREAL). The bus maintenance deficiencies were identified as the possible causes of these fires. The bus operating company, Keolis Compiegne, stated that it would take all necessary measures to correct these deficiencies and ensure passenger safety. The authorities also requested the company to provide an action plan to prevent such situations in the future. The audit was conducted after several fires on Compiegne buses, which caused property damage and service disruptions.
