### Fuel in Seine-Saint-Denis: Where can I fill up with petrol or diesel at the best price this Monday, October 19th?


On October 19th in Seine-Saint-Denis, it is possible to buy fuel at a competitive price by following the following tips:

1. Compare prices at gas stations online before you go. Websites such as Prix Carburant or Gasoline Alerte allow you to compare the prices of different gas stations in the area.
2. Prefer gas stations located outside the city center, as prices are often lower on the outskirts.
3. Use a fuel discount card if you have one. Some cards offer discounts on fuel purchases.
4. Do not let your tank run empty before buying fuel, as gas stations may offer a higher price for customers who need a full tank.
5. Prefer gas stations that offer promotions or occasional discounts on fuel.
6. Do not buy fuel at the end of the week, as prices are often higher at this time.
7. Do not use a gas station that offers additional services such as car washes or oil changes, as these services can increase the price of fuel.
8. Prefer gas stations that offer fixed prices rather than variable prices, as variable prices can fluctuate quickly and be higher.
