### Online violence and psychological distress: A Grenoblois testifies to the harsh reality of the work of moderators


{% include figure.html
    src="https://www.ici.fr/pikapi/images/bb34a7ed-6b05-4ef6-9212-eab4bc9c498e/920x517?webp=false"
    alt="Online violence and psychological distress: A Grenoblois testifies to the harsh reality of the work of moderators"
    caption="Online violence and psychological distress: A Grenoblois testifies to the harsh reality of the work of moderators" %}


The online moderation job is a sensitive issue that has been highlighted by Léa Mendoza, a resident of Grenoble. She wants to raise public awareness of the psychological suffering of social media moderators. Her sister worked for a Meta provider and committed suicide after experiencing psychological trauma related to her work.

Moderators are responsible for monitoring the content posted on social media platforms, removing content that violates these platforms' rules. However, their work is often difficult and stressful, as they regularly have to read violent, abusive, or pornographic messages.

Léa Mendoza testified that her brother was particularly affected by this work, which caused him serious psychological trauma. She believes that companies should take into account the mental health of their employees and implement measures to protect moderators from the negative effects of their work.

This case has sparked a strong reaction in the workplace, with calls to improve the protection of moderators and to recognize their work as a distinct profession. Social media companies have also been invited to consider the psychological consequences for their employees and to implement preventive and support measures.
