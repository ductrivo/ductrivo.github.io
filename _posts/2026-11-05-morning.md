### Why is the motorway area near Montélimar called 'Le Bras de Zil'?


{% include figure.html
    src="https://www.ici.fr/pikapi/images/4f02f440-cff9-46a0-9822-b986565df949/920x517?webp=false"
    alt="Why is the motorway area near Montélimar called 'Le Bras de Zil'?"
    caption="Why is the motorway area near Montélimar called 'Le Bras de Zil'?" %}


The name of the motorway rest area located near Montélimar, known as "Bras de Zil," has long intrigued motorists. This name actually dates back to the late 19th century, well before the era of motorways. Daphné Michelas, a historian specializing in Provençal toponymy, revealed that the name "Bras de Zil" was already used to designate this area at that time. It is possible that the name originated from an old agricultural property or a river that flows into the Drôme, but the exact origins of the name remain a matter of debate. Nevertheless, "Bras de Zil" remains an iconic location on the A7 motorway in Provence-Alpes-Côte d'Azur.
