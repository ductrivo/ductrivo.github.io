### Why is there a street named 'Rue du Mauvais Payeur' (Street of the Bad Payers) in Montélimar?


{% include figure.html
    src="https://www.ici.fr/pikapi/images/79653ed8-84fb-41ca-a7b5-a4b26bcaa6aa/920x517?webp=false"
    alt="Why is there a street named 'Rue du Mauvais Payeur' (Street of the Bad Payers) in Montélimar?"
    caption="Why is there a street named 'Rue du Mauvais Payeur' (Street of the Bad Payers) in Montélimar?" %}


The Rue des Mauvais-Payeurs (Street of the Bad Payers) in Montélimar is named so in memory of a time when merchants tried to avoid the octroi, a tax levied on certain goods entering the city. This practice was common in the Middle Ages and allowed merchants to reduce their costs. Although this practice is now illegal, the name of the street has been preserved as a small local story that has left its mark on the toponymy of Montélimar.

*[Source](https://www.ici.fr/emissions/mais-pourquoi-daphne/pourquoi-trouve-t-on-une-rue-du-mauvais-payeur-a-montelimar-6029322?at_medium=lien_RSS&at_locale=24)*
