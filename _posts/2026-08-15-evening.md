### Solar eclipse of August 12: our selection of events organized in Isère, Savoie, and Haute-Savoie to observe this rare phenomenon


{% include figure.html
    src="https://france3-regions.franceinfo.fr/image/1sw_TQOEcKdjB1pa_0FNhP_GPXo/930x620/filters:format(webp)/regions/2026/08/07/6a75d56581e79040763177.jpg"
    alt="Solar eclipse of August 12: our selection of events organized in Isère, Savoie, and Haute-Savoie to observe this rare phenomenon"
    caption="Solar eclipse of August 12: our selection of events organized in Isère, Savoie, and Haute-Savoie to observe this rare phenomenon" %}


A solar eclipse will be visible from France on August 12th, with a lunar eclipse of 90 to 95% of the sun's disk, depending on the location in the Alps. Here is a selection of events organized in Isère, Haute-Savoie, and Savoie to observe this rare astronomical phenomenon:

* Isère: The Saint-Martin-d'Hères Observatory is hosting a special evening with lectures, video projections, and secure viewing equipment.
* Haute-Savoie: The Meudon Observatory is organizing a day of observation at the Chablais Observatory in Thonon-les-Bains, with lectures and secure viewing equipment.
* Savoie: The Lake Annecy Astronomy Center is hosting a special evening with lectures, video projections, and secure viewing equipment.

It is important to note that direct observation of a solar eclipse without protective equipment can be dangerous for the eyes. It is recommended to use special glasses or polarized glass filters to protect your eyes.
