### A roof against presence: intergenerational living in the Drôme Ardèche region with Solidarité Habitats


{% include figure.html
    src="https://www.ici.fr/pikapi/images/f5cd5082-cad9-444e-8c41-2cb1eacbfc2b/920x517?webp=false"
    alt="A roof against presence: intergenerational living in the Drôme Ardèche region with Solidarité Habitats"
    caption="A roof against presence: intergenerational living in the Drôme Ardèche region with Solidarité Habitats" %}


Intergenerational living is organized by the Solidarité Habitats association in the Drôme Ardèche region for almost 20 years. This system connects hosts of all ages with people who have a professional project. The goal is to create a supportive community where generations share their skills, experience, and living space.

This initiative aims to solve two problems: the shortage of housing for young professionals and the isolation of the elderly. Hosts provide housing to someone in exchange for their help in daily life or their professional skills.

The Solidarité Habitats association provides technical and financial support to the parties involved to facilitate this intergenerational living. It also provides mediation between the parties to resolve any potential conflicts.

This system has already created more than 100 intergenerational living arrangements in the Drôme Ardèche region, with a success rate of almost 90%. The parties involved have reported both material and intangible benefits, such as improved quality of life and deeper relationships.

This initiative is an example of intergenerational cooperation to solve common social problems. It also shows that solidarity can be put into practice in everyday life, thanks to an association like Solidarité Habitats, which facilitates this intergenerational living.
