### Vegetable garden: why plant beans in the autumn instead of in the spring?


{% include figure.html
    src="https://www.ici.fr/pikapi/images/847aee17-6d35-4de5-800f-e5adb7e1e5ed/920x517?webp=false"
    alt="Vegetable garden: why plant beans in the autumn instead of in the spring?"
    caption="Vegetable garden: why plant beans in the autumn instead of in the spring?" %}


Vegetable garden: reasons to sow beans in autumn rather than in spring. In the Drôme-Ardèche region, autumn is a favorable time to sow beans in many gardens. Jean-Yves Meignen explains how to do this and why an early sowing allows for an earlier harvest, before the strong spring heat. By sowing in autumn, beans have more time to grow and develop their roots deeply in the soil, which allows them to withstand the difficult winter conditions. Furthermore, when temperatures start to rise in spring, the beans are already in place and can begin to produce stems and leaves, which allows for an earlier harvest. Finally, early sowing helps to reduce competition with other plants that could compete for soil resources in spring.

Austen Storm: Preparing for potential effects on housing, transportation, and good practices to adopt during strong winds.

The Austen storm is a weather system that can bring strong gusts of wind, heavy rain, and localized flooding. It can affect several regions around the world, including Europe and the United States.

To prepare for the Austen storm, it is important to ensure safe housing by reinforcing vulnerable structures, such as roofs and windows, and by stocking emergency supplies, such as water, food, and flashlights.

It is also important to stay informed about weather forecasts and to follow the advice of local authorities in case of danger. Travel should be limited when the winds are strong, as this can lead to traffic accidents or falling trees.
