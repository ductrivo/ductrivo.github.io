### Social networks: The Constitutional Council censures the ban for those under 15


{% include figure.html
    src="https://www.ici.fr/pikapi/images/8804ef71-19de-45fd-bcc7-cfb3911a7d90/920x517?webp=false"
    alt="Social networks: The Constitutional Council censures the ban for those under 15"
    caption="Social networks: The Constitutional Council censures the ban for those under 15" %}


The Constitutional Council annulled last Friday the ban imposed by the law that prohibited minors under 15 from accessing social media, effective September 1st. The decision was due to the fact that this ban would have violated the fundamental rights of children regarding freedom of expression and communication. The government was invited to revise the law to make it conform to constitutional principles, which should be done "by spring 2027". Emmanuel Macron tasked Sébastien Lecornu, Minister of National Education, with rewriting the text.

The authorities have therefore decided to act in collaboration with legitimate local businesses to find a sustainable solution to this problem. The measures taken include the creation of commercial zones reserved for legitimate businesses and the improvement of working conditions for these businesses.

In conclusion, the fight against street vendors in Vanves is a difficult and ongoing task for local authorities. However, by collaborating with legitimate local businesses, they hope to find a sustainable solution to this problem.

### Soon, a 'Richter scale' for fires will be developed to 'understand their real impact'?


A project to develop a Richter scale specific to fires is underway. The goal is to measure the actual impact of fires, which would allow for better prediction and risk management. This new scale would be based on parameters such as the size of the burned area, the duration of the fire, the extent of material and environmental damage, as well as human consequences. It could also help to compare fires with each other and to identify the factors that can contribute to their intensity or spread. However, it is important to note that the original Richter scale, used to measure earthquakes, cannot be applied to fires because it does not take into account the specific factors related to fires.
