### 'There were dozens at every step': what are these animals that have washed up by the hundreds on the beaches of the Morbihan?


The text "There were at every step" refers to the unexpected and tragic event that occurred in 2016 on the beaches of the Morbihan, in France. Hundreds of marine animals were found stranded on the coast, primarily cetaceans (whales and dolphins), but also seabirds.

The causes of this event are still not fully understood, but several hypotheses have been proposed. It is possible that it was related to a mass mortality of fish that attracted the animals to the coast, or to unfavorable weather conditions that disrupted their migration.

This event has attracted significant media attention and mobilized thousands of volunteers to rescue the stranded animals and remove their bodies. Local authorities have also taken measures to protect the local marine fauna, including temporarily banning human activities in certain coastal areas.

Unfortunately, most of the stranded animals did not survive their experience on land. Experts have pointed out that these events are frequent, and that cetaceans are often victims of bad weather or human interference.

This event has also highlighted the importance of volunteers in protecting marine fauna, as well as the need for a better understanding of the causes of these events in order to prevent their recurrence.
