### Sweden: the age of criminal responsibility lowered to 14 years


In Sweden, the age of criminal responsibility has been lowered to 14 years. Before this reform, only minors aged 15 and older could be prosecuted for criminal offenses. This measure came into effect on January 1, 2020.

This decision was made in order to improve the protection of victims and reduce recidivism rates. Minors aged 14 to 15 will be prioritized by specialized services, rather than the traditional penal system.

This reform has sparked some controversy in Sweden. Some have criticized the decision to move towards a more lenient system, while others have emphasized the importance of taking into account the specific needs of minors and providing them with preventive and rehabilitative assistance.

It should be noted that Sweden is not the only European country to lower the age of criminal responsibility. For example, in Germany, minors aged 14 can be prosecuted for criminal offenses.

This reform in Sweden is an important step in the fight against crime and the protection of victims, but it remains subject to debate and observation of its effects on Swedish society.
