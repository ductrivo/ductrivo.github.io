### Romans-sur-Isère. A network for selling blood glucose sensors has been dismantled in the Drôme and Isère departments.


{% include figure.html
    src="https://cdn-s-www.ledauphine.com/images/1FAA5EA1-3555-41EF-8EE6-3319CFE2326E/NW_listB/un-trafic-de-capteurs-de-glycemie-demantele-dans-la-drome-et-l-isere-1787670688.jpg"
    alt="Romans-sur-Isère. A network for selling blood glucose sensors has been dismantled in the Drôme and Isère departments."
    caption="Romans-sur-Isère. A network for selling blood glucose sensors has been dismantled in the Drôme and Isère departments." %}


In the Rhône-Alpes region, an illegal network for selling blood glucose meters was dismantled in Romans-sur-Isère, in the Drôme and Isère departments. The scheme was complex: people with diabetes were approached to buy affordable blood glucose meters. However, the devices were fake or unusable, which had serious consequences for those involved.

Investigators discovered that the network was organized by a group of traffickers operating in several cities. They also identified a number of suspects and seized thousands of fake devices. Authorities have implemented measures to protect patients with diabetes and to prevent the re-emergence of illegal trafficking.

This case highlights the importance of verifying the authenticity of medical products, especially those intended for people with chronic conditions such as diabetes. Authorities encourage patients to consult their doctor before buying a blood glucose meter and to ensure its authenticity.

### Why are the Cévénol episodes likely to be particularly violent this year?


{% include figure.html
    src="https://www.ici.fr/pikapi/images/f43f77c5-c681-4d14-858b-b96114851c1b/920x517?webp=false"
    alt="Why are the Cévénol episodes likely to be particularly violent this year?"
    caption="Why are the Cévénol episodes likely to be particularly violent this year?" %}


Cévénol episodes, a meteorological phenomenon characterized by heavy rains and flooding in the Cévennes mountains, are likely to be particularly intense this year due to several climate factors. The last days of August saw an increase in severe weather in the Gard and Lozère regions, with heavy rainfall. The sea is also unusually warm, which can contribute to intensifying Cévénol episodes by causing violent storms. Furthermore, dry soils, resulting from a particularly hot summer, are likely to amplify the effects of rainfall, creating a risk of landslides and flooding. Therefore, the meteorological authorities have warned the population about the high risk of severe flooding in this region in the coming weeks.
