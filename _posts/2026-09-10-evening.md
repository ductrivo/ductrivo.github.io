In Romans, Daphné Michelas recounts the golden age of music kiosks, explaining their role in French cities. Today, there are very few music kiosks left in France, but their history continues to be remembered and appreciated by enthusiasts of cultural heritage.

### Why can you find the 'Pont des Étoiles' in Rompon?


{% include figure.html
    src="https://www.ici.fr/pikapi/images/02fe0173-8aee-4e34-8de2-05e1625d9394/920x517?webp=false"
    alt="Why can you find the 'Pont des Étoiles' in Rompon?"
    caption="Why can you find the 'Pont des Étoiles' in Rompon?" %}


The Pont des Étoiles (Stars Bridge) is located between Rompon and La Voulte-sur-Rhône, in the Ardèche region. This bridge got its name due to the discovery of marine fossils dating back 165 million years. Daphné Michelas guides us through this discovery. The fossils were found on the pillars of the bridge, resembling starfish, which gave the bridge its name. Built in 1879, it allowed the passage of the Paris-Lyon-Marseille railway line. Today, it is an interesting tourist destination for those interested in historical and natural discoveries.

*[Source](https://www.ici.fr/emissions/mais-pourquoi-daphne/pourquoi-a-rompon-trouve-t-on-le-pont-des-etoiles-4287591?at_medium=lien_RSS&at_locale=24)*
