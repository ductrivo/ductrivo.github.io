The mayor of Cannes announced that the city will equip its municipal police with nitrogen oxide (NOx) detectors, which would be a first in France. This measure is being taken to combat polluting emissions from vehicles, particularly in the city center, which is known for its heavy traffic and numerous demonstrations. The detectors will be used to identify vehicles emitting high levels of NOx, allowing municipal police officers to take corrective measures, such as fines or technical inspections.
