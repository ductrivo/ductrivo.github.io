### Gloomy


Mortality rate, or specific mortality, is a statistical measure used to assess the probability of a person of a given age dying within a specified period of time. It is calculated by dividing the number of deaths within a given population by the total number of people in that population during the same period of time. Mortality rate can be used to compare mortality rates between different populations or to track mortality trends within a specific population. It is often expressed as deaths per 100,000 people per year (standardized mortality). Factors that influence mortality include age, sex, race, health behaviors, socio-economic conditions, and environmental factors. Mortality rate can be used to identify the main causes of death in a population, to determine the mortality risks associated with certain diseases or health conditions, and to evaluate the effectiveness of mortality prevention programs.
