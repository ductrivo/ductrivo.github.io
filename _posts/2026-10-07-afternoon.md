### Why are the rocky peaks overlooking Saint-Nizier-du-Moucherotte called the 'Three Virgins'?


{% include figure.html
    src="https://www.ici.fr/pikapi/images/3cd053c5-f028-40ab-8bad-b87afcb5447b/920x517?webp=false"
    alt="Why are the rocky peaks overlooking Saint-Nizier-du-Moucherotte called the 'Three Virgins'?"
    caption="Why are the rocky peaks overlooking Saint-Nizier-du-Moucherotte called the 'Three Virgins'?" %}


The rocky peaks overlooking Saint-Nizier-du-Moucherotte are known as the Three Virgins. The origin of this name is subject to various legends and anecdotes, which give the area a certain mystique.

One of the most popular stories tells that these rocks were once witches turned to stone by monks to prevent them from harming the villagers. Another legend claims that the three virgins were actually martyred virgins during the Roman period, who were turned to stone to protect them from barbarians.

A more recent anecdote tells that these rocks were used as a landmark by local shepherds to guide their flocks. They are said to have given these rocks the name "Three Virgins" because of their shape, which resembled the headdresses of virgins.

Finally, there is a more scientific theory that suggests that the Three Virgins are simply natural rock formations, whose shape was created by erosion and wind.

Something mysterious and romantic surrounds these three virgins, which continue to attract visitors to Saint-Nizier-du-Moucherotte.

*[Source](https://www.ici.fr/emissions/tout-s-explique-ici-isere/pourquoi-les-aiguilles-rocheuses-qui-surplombent-saint-nizier-du-moucherotte-s-appellent-les-3-pucelles-7524273?at_medium=lien_RSS&at_locale=29)*

### 'No one cared about me': Sylvester Stallone recounts his struggles before 'Rocky' in a moving book.
