### Keep carrots and beets all winter long thanks to the silo technique.


{% include figure.html
    src="https://www.ici.fr/pikapi/images/a9e17e99-b7cb-457d-a7a4-c0aba739596b/920x517?webp=false"
    alt="Keep carrots and beets all winter long thanks to the silo technique."
    caption="Keep carrots and beets all winter long thanks to the silo technique." %}


Storing carrots, parsnips, and beets during the winter can be achieved using the silo technique. This method involves storing these vegetables in a closed space, typically underground or semi-underground, filled with damp sand.

By storing the vegetables in damp sand, their freshness is preserved while freeing the garden soil for the winter. This technique ensures a local and sustainable food supply during the cold season.

The silo can be built from various materials such as brick, wood, or even plastic containers. It is important to ensure that the interior space is well-ventilated to prevent spoilage and thus maintain the vegetables fresh for several months.

The silo technique is a traditional method of vegetable preservation, which prevents the degradation of products due to cold or humidity. It is also environmentally friendly as it does not require the use of chemicals to preserve the vegetables.

In conclusion, the silo technique is an effective and environmentally friendly method for storing carrots, parsnips, and beets during the winter. It allows for the release of garden soil while ensuring a local and sustainable food supply during the cold season.
