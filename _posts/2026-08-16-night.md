Alpes-de-Haute-Provence: Discovering Stars and Galaxies

The Saint-Michel-l'Observatoire astronomy center is a unique location in the Alpes-de-Haute-Provence, France. It offers a unique opportunity to discover the beauty of the night sky and the celestial constellations thanks to its telescopes and planetarium.

Telescopes of different sizes allow visitors to see stars, galaxies, and other celestial objects in detail. The center also has a sidereal telescope that allows you to watch the Sun in real time, without risking your eyesight.

The center's planetarium is another highlight, offering an immersive and interactive experience for visitors. It allows you to discover stars, galaxies, and celestial phenomena in three dimensions, thanks to projections on a 12-meter diameter sphere.

The Saint-Michel-l'Observatoire astronomy center is also equipped with a solar observatory, which allows visitors to discover the Sun in detail, thanks to specialized telescopes.

The center also offers guided tours and workshops for children, in order to introduce the science of astronomy to the youngest. It is open all year round and offers an educational and entertaining experience for all ages.

### European Swimming Championships: Unbeatable, Léon Marchand wins his second European title in the 400m freestyle.


The European Swimming Championships recently concluded, with Léon Marchand winning the 400-meter freestyle event. This is his second consecutive European title in this discipline, after winning in 2018 in Glasgow. With his two gold medals, Léon Marchand confirms his position as a leading European swimmer in his favorite event and is preparing to continue his career with ambition for the 2021 Tokyo Olympics.
