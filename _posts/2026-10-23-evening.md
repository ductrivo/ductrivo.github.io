    caption="Why wasn't the Ardèche department called 'Ardèche' when it was created?" %}


The Ardèche department, created in 1790 during the French Revolution, did not immediately adopt its current name upon creation. Initially associated with the Vivarais, the former historical territory, it eventually adopted the name of its river, as part of the country's administrative reorganization.
