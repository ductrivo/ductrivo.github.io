### Martine Rolland of Grenoble, the first high-mountain guide in Europe's history


Martine Rolland, born in Grenoble, was the first high-mountain guide in Europe's history. She broke barriers and made history in 1932 by becoming the first female high-mountain guide in Europe. Her remarkable career began at the age of 20 when she joined the French Alpine Club and began leading expeditions in the French Alps. In 1936, she reached her highest peak, Mont Blanc du Tacul, as the first woman to climb this mountain via the normal route. Rolland was also the first woman to climb many other prestigious peaks in the French and Italian Alps, including the Grand Capucin and Mont Rose. She continued to lead expeditions until 1950, when she retired due to health reasons. Martine Rolland is considered a pioneer in mountaineering, and her contribution to the sport is recognized today.

*[Source](https://www.ici.fr/emissions/les-illustres-iserois/la-grenobloise-martine-rolland-la-premiere-guide-de-haute-montagne-d-europe-de-l-histoire-2778603?at_medium=lien_RSS&at_locale=29)*
