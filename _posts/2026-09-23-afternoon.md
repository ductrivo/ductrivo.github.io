### Why is the old Ségoins sawmill in Valjouffrey called a 'hammer mill'?


{% include figure.html
    src="https://www.ici.fr/pikapi/images/c6307481-a23d-4535-b71c-ace0943744eb/920x517?webp=false"
    alt="Why is the old Ségoins sawmill in Valjouffrey called a 'hammer mill'?"
    caption="Why is the old Ségoins sawmill in Valjouffrey called a 'hammer mill'?" %}


The Ségoins sawmill in Valjouffrey, built in 1904, is known as a "band saw mill". After being abandoned for three decades, it was brought back to life twenty years ago thanks to the joint efforts of the municipality and an association. As part of the "ICI Isère" project, a short radio tour is offered for a journey back in time. The Ségoins sawmill was initially equipped with a band saw, which used the hydraulic power of the river to cut logs into planks. This technique was more efficient and less expensive than other existing sawing methods at the time. Today, the Ségoins sawmill has been transformed into a cultural and tourist center, where visitors can discover the history of the region and the work of the sawmill.

### DIRECT. Mosquitoes: Why are there so many? What attracts them? How can we protect ourselves? Ask us your questions.


Title: Mosquitoes: Why are they so numerous? What attracts them? How to protect yourself?

The mosquito is an insect of the order Diptera, common in all regions of the world. It is known for being the main vector of diseases such as yellow fever, malaria, and dengue.

Mosquitoes are attracted to humidity, heat, and human odors, particularly those emitted by sweat, saliva, and even breathing. They can also be attracted to light colors, such as white or yellow.

To protect yourself from mosquitoes, it is recommended to wear long-sleeved clothing and repelents, use chemical repellent creams on the skin, and use protective devices such as incense lamps or mosquito traps. It is also important to protect yourself from mosquitoes when traveling in tropical or subtropical areas, where the risk of diseases transmitted by these insects is higher.
