### Falling student achievement: how to get things back on track


Title: Improving Student Academic Performance: How to Overcome the Challenge

The increasing rates of student failure and declining academic results are a growing concern in education. To reverse this trend, a comprehensive approach is needed that addresses multiple factors.

First, it is important to implement educational programs that are tailored to the individual needs of students. This may include the implementation of individualized instruction, the use of innovative learning methods, and the provision of appropriate teaching resources.

Second, it is essential to promote a culture of learning in schools. This can be done by encouraging students to take responsibility for their learning and by helping them to develop teamwork skills.

In addition, it is important to provide psychological support to students who are struggling academically. This may include the implementation of counseling services for students and their families, as well as the organization of individual reflection sessions to help students overcome their difficulties.

Finally, it is necessary to implement regular monitoring and evaluation measures to measure student progress and identify areas for improvement. This may include the implementation of regular assessments, the use of statistical analysis software, and the provision of regular reports to parents.

In conclusion, to improve student academic performance, a comprehensive approach is needed that includes tailored educational programs, a culture of learning in schools, psychological support for students, and regular monitoring measures.

### At the Grand Palais, this exhibition places Cézanne as the patron saint of painting for the past 150 years.


On the occasion of an exhibition at the Grand Palais, Cézanne is honored as the patron of painting for the last 150 years. The exhibition, titled "Cézanne and the Impressionists," brings together over 250 works by the French artist, as well as those of his contemporaries such as Monet, Renoir, and Van Gogh. It highlights Cézanne's major contribution to the evolution of landscape and nude painting in Western art. The exhibition is organized by the Musée d'Orsay and the Grand Palais des Champs-Élysées, in collaboration with the Musée national de Cézanne d'Aix-en-Provence. It takes place from October 23, 2019 to January 26, 2020.
