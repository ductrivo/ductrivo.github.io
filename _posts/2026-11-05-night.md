### Project to store energy using lithium batteries on farmland sparks controversy in the Beauce region


The proposed project involves installing lithium-based energy storage systems on agricultural land in the Beauce region. This initiative has sparked a strong reaction from local farmers, who see their livelihoods threatened by this new installation. The batteries are intended to store excess electricity generated by solar and wind power plants for use when demand exceeds production. However, farmers are concerned about the loss of arable land, the negative environmental impacts, and the risks associated with the transportation and storage of toxic materials. Project proponents claim that the batteries are necessary to achieve energy transition goals and that their environmental impact will be limited through protective and waste management measures. They also promise to offer financial compensation to farmers affected by the loss of arable land. Discussions between stakeholders are ongoing to find a solution acceptable to all.

*[Source](https://www.leparisien.fr/eure-et-loir-28/le-projet-de-stockage-denergie-par-batteries-au-lithium-sur-des-terres-agricoles-enflamme-la-beauce-05-11-2026-TC3VXZG4P5FVBOQNEUPA5SOUUQ.php)*
