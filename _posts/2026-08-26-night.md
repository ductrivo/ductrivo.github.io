### Chikungunya case in Nogent-sur-Marne: we conducted a door-to-door campaign to find the 'patient zero'


In France, an outbreak of chikungunya has been detected in Nogent-sur-Marne. Health authorities have conducted a door-to-door operation to identify the first confirmed case, known as "patient zero." The goal is to understand the origin and progression of the outbreak. Chikungunya is a viral disease transmitted by mosquito vectors, which can cause symptoms such as fever, joint pain, and rheumatic pain, as well as a feeling of fatigue. Preventive measures include eliminating stagnant water around homes to reduce the mosquito vector population. Those affected are advised to stay hydrated and consult a doctor if their symptoms become severe or persistent. The outbreak is currently under investigation to determine additional measures to control its spread.
