### Another theft of hafnium crystals, a strategic metal, at the Framatome factory in Jarrie, which is classified as a Seveso site.


{% include figure.html
    src="https://www.ici.fr/pikapi/images/dee50773-11a4-4d01-ac1d-7723c9632ca0/920x517?webp=false"
    alt="Another theft of hafnium crystals, a strategic metal, at the Framatome factory in Jarrie, which is classified as a Seveso site."
    caption="Another theft of hafnium crystals, a strategic metal, at the Framatome factory in Jarrie, which is classified as a Seveso site." %}


The theft of hafnium crystals from the Framatome factory in Jarrie, which is classified as a Seveso site, involved the theft of several hundred kilos of the material during the night from Wednesday to Thursday. This information was confirmed by Dauphiné Libéré and ICI Isère. Hafnium is a strategic metal used in the aerospace and nuclear industries. The Framatome factory in Jarrie, located in Isère, is classified as a Seveso site due to the presence of dangerous materials on its premises. Authorities have been informed of the theft, and investigations are underway to identify the perpetrators and recover the stolen hafnium crystals.

### 'The pack has been unleashed': The ordeal of another Kevin T., wrongly portrayed as the killer of Trappes on social media.


The title "The pack has been unleashed" refers to an event that occurred in France, where a person named Kevin T. was falsely identified as the killer of Trappes on social media.

Kevin T., who had no connection to the case, was harassed and threatened by thousands of internet users due to his name being similar to that of the actual suspect, Kevin N.

The confusion arose when images of an arrest of Kevin T., carried out for a different case, were posted on social media and associated with the Trappes case.

Kevin T. was forced to quit his job and change his address to escape the threats. He also filed a lawsuit against a journalist who had published false information about him.

The police finally arrested Kevin N., the real suspect, who confessed to committing the murders in Trappes.

This event highlighted the power of social media to spread misinformation and the need for better source verification before publishing information on social media.
