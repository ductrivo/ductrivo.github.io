{% include figure.html

    alt="What is the connection between the pierced stone of Matheysine, the Duke of Lesdiguières, and the devil?"
    caption="What is the connection between the pierced stone of Matheysine, the Duke of Lesdiguières, and the devil?" %}


The "Pierced Rock" of Matheysine, located in the Massif Central in France, is known for its unique and spectacular shape. According to geologists, this formation is due to the action of glaciers and natural erosion. However, an old local legend proposes a different explanation. According to this legend, the Duke of Lesdiguières, a French hero of the 17th century, made a pact with the devil to defeat his enemies. The devil then gave him the strength necessary for his victory, but he also demanded a "pierced rock" as proof of the pact's existence. Lesdiguières then found the "Pierced Rock" in the mountains, and the devil may have taken it after the Duke had fulfilled his part of the agreement. This legend has no scientific basis, but it is still popular in the region.
