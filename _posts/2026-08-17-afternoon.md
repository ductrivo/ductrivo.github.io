### 'We've all been raised on this delicious pastry': the bakery from Mens is celebrating its 100th anniversary.


{% include figure.html
    src="https://www.ici.fr/pikapi/images/03097841-150a-4df4-814b-39cef4c365f4/920x517?webp=false"
    alt="'We've all been raised on this delicious pastry': the bakery from Mens is celebrating its 100th anniversary."
    caption="'We've all been raised on this delicious pastry': the bakery from Mens is celebrating its 100th anniversary." %}


La Bouffette, a famous pastry originating from Mens in France, celebrated its 100th anniversary on Sunday, August 16th. Created in 1926 by the Perrier bakery, this delicious specialty is now known for its unique taste and characteristic candle shape.

The Bouffette is made from a dough based on flour, sugar, butter, and eggs, covered with a sweet glaze. It is traditionally decorated with a chocolate flame on top.

For pastry lovers, this year was an opportunity to learn more about the process of making the Bouffette and to purchase original examples. Visitors were able to attend demonstrations and ask questions of experienced bakers.

The Bouffette is now an integral part of French gastronomy, and its popularity continues to grow. It is often offered at parties and special events, and is considered a symbol of French culinary tradition.

This year will therefore mark an important milestone in the history of the Bouffette, and lovers of this delicious pastry can expect special events to celebrate its centennial.

The book focuses on the encounter between Pelé and the Yves-du-Manoir stadium, when the legendary Brazilian footballer played a match against the French team in 1975. This encounter is considered one of the most important in French football history.

The book also features the memories of prominent athletes such as Michel Platini, Zinédine Zidane, and Didier Deschamps, who played in this stadium during their careers. They share their personal experiences and anecdotes about the stadium, as well as its technical and historical characteristics.

The book "Pelé Played There!" is a unique testament to the great moments of French football and the history of the Yves-du-Manoir stadium. It offers readers an intimate and personal view of these historical events, as well as an appreciation of the cultural and sporting significance of the stadium for the French.
