### Why does a prestigious Saint-Émilion wine called Château la Tour-du-Pin get its name?


{% include figure.html
    src="https://www.ici.fr/pikapi/images/e33789b5-39e0-43a0-bbda-47883ec3e9fa/920x517?webp=false"
    alt="Why does a prestigious Saint-Émilion wine called Château la Tour-du-Pin get its name?"
    caption="Why does a prestigious Saint-Émilion wine called Château la Tour-du-Pin get its name?" %}


Château la Tour-du-Pin is a Grand Cru of Saint-Emilion, one of the most prestigious wine appellations in France. Its name is directly linked to the Isère, a region located in south-east France.

To understand this connection, we must go back to the French Revolution. At that time, a certain Jean-Baptiste de La Tour du Pin, a nobleman from the Isère, bought land in the Saint-Emilion region. He built a chateau and vineyards, which he named Château la Tour-du-Pin in reference to his own name.

After the Revolution, the property of nobles was confiscated and sold at public auctions. Château la Tour-du-Pin thus changed hands several times before being bought by a family of wine merchants from Bordeaux in the 19th century.

Today, Château la Tour-du-Pin is considered one of the best wines of Saint-Emilion and is classified as a Grand Cru. Its name remains a testament to the history of the French Revolution and the region of the Isère.
