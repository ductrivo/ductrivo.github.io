### But why did the Cordeliers Chapel in Valencia serve for everything except praying?


{% include figure.html
    src="https://www.ici.fr/pikapi/images/8996d65b-45b1-451b-a0a4-158db5efc7d5/920x517?webp=false"
    alt="But why did the Cordeliers Chapel in Valencia serve for everything except praying?"
    caption="But why did the Cordeliers Chapel in Valencia serve for everything except praying?" %}


The Cordeliers Chapel in Valence has a turbulent history. Initially built in the 13th century to house Franciscan monks, it served various purposes after the French Revolution. In the 19th century, it was transformed into a prison, then used as a military store and a reception hall. In 1926, it became the departmental archives of Drôme. Today, it is an important historical site in the city of Valence.

The animal, named the Pampas Chaco feline, was identified through genetic analysis and compared to other South American feline species. It resembles the jaguarundi but is larger and has lighter fur. The Pampas Chaco feline lives in the wetlands and wooded areas of the Beni region, where it preys on small mammals and birds. The discovery of this new feline species highlights the importance of conserving fragile and threatened habitats around the world.
