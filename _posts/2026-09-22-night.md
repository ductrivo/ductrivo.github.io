The French government announced on May 16 measures to help French citizens cope with rising fuel prices. These measures include expanding assistance for long-distance drivers, which will affect approximately 5.5 million people. This assistance consists of a reduction in income tax rates for households with an annual income of less than 24,000 euros and annual fuel expenses of more than 1,500 euros. This measure is scheduled to last until the end of 2022.
