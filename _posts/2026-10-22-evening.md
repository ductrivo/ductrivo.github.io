### A man died in France after contracting the Shamonda virus, which was first detected in humans.


{% include figure.html

    alt="A man died in France after contracting the Shamonda virus, which was first detected in humans."
    caption="A man died in France after contracting the Shamonda virus, which was first detected in humans." %}


A man died in France after contracting the Shamonda virus, which was detected in humans for the first time. The immunocompromised patient died in early October in France, according to French authorities. No public health risk has been identified to date. The Shamonda virus was initially detected in animals in Africa and Southeast Asia. It is a virus of the Paramyxoviridae family, which also includes the measles and influenza viruses. French authorities have stated that they are in contact with international organizations to investigate the origin of the case and to assess the measures to be taken.
