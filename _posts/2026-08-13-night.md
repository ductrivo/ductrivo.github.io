### The 4th edition of the Grain de Sable Festival

    caption="La 4e édition du Festival Grain de Sable" %}


The 4th edition of the Grain de Sable Festival is about to take place. This festival offers a family-friendly, friendly, and accessible live performance. It is known for its varied program, including theatrical performances, concerts, exhibitions of visual artists, and outdoor activities.

The festival is distinguished by its eco-friendly commitment. The organizers have implemented several measures to reduce their environmental impact, such as the use of recyclable materials, the limitation of food waste, and the encouragement of sustainable transportation.

In addition to the artistic program, the festival also offers educational activities for children, such as art workshops and eco-friendly games. There is also a camping area for visitors who wish to stay on site for several days.

The Grain de Sable Festival is a unique cultural event that brings together artists, audiences, and nature lovers in an idyllic setting. It offers a unique opportunity for people of all ages and backgrounds to share unforgettable moments in nature.

### The Sautet lake via ferrata: a thrilling experience between Drac, the mountain, and the industrial world.

    src="https://www.ici.fr/pikapi/images/8b263bd0-6262-4d4d-a7b7-c5e361c9681b/920x517?webp=false"

The Lac du Sautet is home to the Grand Frisson via ferrata, an exhilarating experience between the wild gorges and the Drac industrial site. This via ferrata is a vertical route that takes adventurers through spectacular cliffs, offering panoramic views of the valley. The route takes place in an exceptional natural environment, with rocky cliffs and deep gorges, all while passing near a historic hydroelectric plant. The Grand Frisson via ferrata is a unique experience that allows visitors to discover the wild nature and the industrial industry of Drac in a single route.
