### 'The best thing to do is to go to a higher altitude': tips for making the most of the solar eclipse in Isère


{% include figure.html
    src="https://www.ici.fr/pikapi/images/f8cb23c8-f250-48fe-81ab-bfaf30825db1/920x517?webp=false"
    alt="'The best thing to do is to go to a higher altitude': tips for making the most of the solar eclipse in Isère"
    caption="'The best thing to do is to go to a higher altitude': tips for making the most of the solar eclipse in Isère" %}


The solar eclipse on Wednesday, August 12, 2026, will be visible throughout France, including in Isère. However, the total obscuration will reach approximately 94%, which means that to make the most of this exceptional event, it is important to go to suitable areas.

For residents of Isère, ICI Isère provides some tips:

1. Locate an area with a high obscuration rate: The cities of Grenoble and Saint-Étienne will not be ideally located to fully enjoy the eclipse, as the obscuration rate will be less than 90%. It is therefore recommended to go to rural or mountainous areas where the obscuration rate will be higher.
2. Prepare your equipment: To observe the eclipse, it is important to have sunglasses specially designed to protect the eyes. It is also recommended to use a projection filter to be able to observe the eclipse on a flat surface.
3. Prepare your stay: Due to the interest generated by this eclipse, it is important to prepare your stay in advance. It is recommended to book accommodation and plan a route to the chosen observation area.
4. Follow the advice of experts: To make the most of the eclipse, it is important to follow the advice of astronomy experts. It is recommended not to look directly at the sun during the eclipse and to follow the safety advice to protect your eyes.
5. Share your experience: The solar eclipse is an exceptional event that occurs only at regular intervals. It is therefore recommended to share this experience with others and to document your observation to preserve a memory of this unique event.

### Should tomato plants be pruned during the summer?


{% include figure.html
    src="https://www.ici.fr/pikapi/images/ad8f530f-a743-4bc0-bb88-23a7ed3461ae/920x517?webp=false"
    alt="Should tomato plants be pruned during the summer?"
    caption="Should tomato plants be pruned during the summer?" %}


Tomatoes are not daisies and do not require pruning like they do. Some gardeners claim that removing leaves from tomato plants would allow more light to reach the fruits, but this is controversial. In fact, the leaves of tomato plants are used for photosynthesis and oxygen production, which is beneficial for fruit development. Furthermore, removing leaves can promote the appearance of diseases or harmful insects. Therefore, it is best not to prune tomato plants during the summer.

### Safran: Our tips for planting your bulbs this summer


{% include figure.html
    src="https://www.ici.fr/pikapi/images/13eeb9a9-778b-46e1-a27b-db915906e643/920x517?webp=false"
    alt="Safran: Our tips for planting your bulbs this summer"
    caption="Safran: Our tips for planting your bulbs this summer" %}


Saffron is a well-known aromatic plant, famous for its bulb, which is used as a spice and medicinal herb. Contrary to popular belief, saffron should not be planted in the autumn, but rather in the spring or summer. Jean-Yves Meignen, a saffron expert, provides advice on how to successfully cultivate this relatively easy-to-grow plant.

For good exposure, saffron prefers a sunny and well-drained location. It is suitable for all types of soil, but it is important to avoid areas with high moisture. The planting density should be 10 bulbs per square meter for commercial cultivation, or 5 bulbs per square meter for home cultivation.

It is important to plant the bulbs at a depth of 5 cm and space them 10 cm apart. After planting, it takes about six weeks for the first shoots to appear.

To ensure optimal saffron growth, it is necessary to avoid over-fertilizing and to regularly loosen the soil. It is also important to protect the bulbs from wildlife by covering them with soil or rose leaves.

Finally, to harvest the flowers in the autumn, it is important not to cut the stems before the flowers are completely dry. The bulbs can be stored for several years and used as a spice or medicinal herb.

By following these tips, you can successfully cultivate your own saffron.

*[Source](https://www.ici.fr/emissions/cote-jardin-ici-drome-ardeche/au-jardin-c-est-le-moment-ideal-pour-planter-le-safran-2046000?at_medium=lien_RSS&at_locale=24)*
