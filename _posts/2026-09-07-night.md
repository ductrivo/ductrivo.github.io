{% include figure.html

    caption="Saint-Rambert-d'Albon. Adrien Hours, a figure of the resistance, has died at the age of 103." %}


Adrien Hours, a prominent figure of the French Resistance, died at the age of 103 on September 1, 2023, in Saint-Rambert-d'Albon. Hours was an active member of the Resistance from the beginning of World War II and played an important role in the fight against the Nazi occupation in France. He is known for his courage and dedication to the cause of the Resistance, as well as for his involvement in sabotage and intelligence activities. Hours was recognized for his service to France and received several honorary awards, including the Croix de Guerre with palm and the Medal of the Resistance. He is considered a national hero of France, and his death was mourned by French authorities and Resistance organizations.

### 'I don't have any money, so the mussels...' Solidarity and anti-waste: the winning recipe for after the Lille flea market.


Title: "I have no money, so mussels..." : Solidarity and anti-waste, the winning recipe after the Lille Flea Market.

The text describes a popular culinary tradition in Lille, France, which takes place after the Lille flea market, a large annual market. The tradition involves eating mussels, a local specialty, when participants no longer have money to buy more beer.

The recipe is called "antigaspi," which means "no waste" in Flemish. The mussels are cooked in a shared pot with white wine, onions, and shallots, and then served with bread to absorb the juice.

The tradition is also associated with solidarity among the flea market participants, as they share mussels and wine in a spirit of camaraderie. People often gather around a common table to eat together.

The text emphasizes that this tradition has become an important part of local culture in Lille, and attracts thousands of participants every year.
