### European Swimming Championships: sleep, video, mental… The key role of data in swimmer preparation


The European Swimming Championships will take place from May 16th to 21st in Budapest, Hungary. The competition brings together the best European swimmers for a week of intense competition.

To prepare for these events, national teams are increasingly using data and digital analysis. Swimmers are monitored by cameras and sensors that collect information on their swimming style, heart rate, breathing rate, muscle strength, and even their mental movements.

This data is then analyzed to identify the strengths and weaknesses of the swimmers, as well as to find optimal training strategies. For example, swimmers may be encouraged to change their swimming style to reduce energy consumption or to improve their mental focus to reduce stress before an important race.

The data is also used to prepare swimmers for the competition conditions, by simulating the conditions of the Budapest pool in training pools equipped with similar filtration and heating systems.

Therefore, digital data is of great importance to swimmers who want to be competitive at the European Swimming Championships. It allows them to prepare optimally, focus on their weaknesses, and achieve their best.

### Near Dieppe, discover the inner workings of the German bunker R 600, in celebration of the Jubilee operation.


Near Dieppe, you can visit the interior of the German bunker R 600 as part of the Jubilee operation commemoration. Built during World War II, this bunker was a major coastal defense point for the Germans. Today, it is open to the public and offers an intimate view of the underwater warfare that took place in this area.

The R 600 bunker is located at the end of Dieppe beach, where Allied troops attempted a landing in August 1942. The Jubilee operation was one of the most important Allied landing attempts on the French coast during the war, but it failed due to German resistance.

The R 600 bunker is a remarkable example of German military architecture from World War II. It was equipped with anti-aircraft and anti-ship weapons, as well as communication and coastal defense control systems. Today, it is preserved as a historical monument and offers a view of the underwater warfare that took place in this area during World War II.

Visiting the R 600 bunker is a unique opportunity to learn about the history of World War II near Dieppe. It also offers a view of the Allied efforts to land in France and liberate Europe from Nazism.

### Solar eclipse on August 12: What time should I look at the sky in Paris?


Solar eclipse of August 12, 2026: What time to look at the sky in Paris?

The total solar eclipse of August 12, 2026, will be visible in France, including Paris. The event will begin around 9:05 AM and last until approximately 11:30 AM. However, the period of total eclipse will only last for a few minutes, between 10:40 AM and 10:45 AM.

To observe the solar eclipse in Paris, it is important to take precautions to protect your eyes. Ordinary sunglasses are not sufficient to view the solar eclipse and can cause irreversible damage to the retina. It is recommended to use special glasses designed for observing solar eclipses or to construct a safety filter using a black glass plate and opaque cardboard.

It is also important to note that the total solar eclipse of August 12, 2026, will be visible in other parts of the world, including North America and Europe. Details on the duration and visibility of the eclipse may vary depending on the region.

Finally, it is recommended that observers gather in groups to observe the solar eclipse, as this will allow them to exchange information and share the experience. It is also important to follow the appropriate health measures according to the global situation at that time.
