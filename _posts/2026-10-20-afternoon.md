### Explosion on Rue de Trévise: The City of Paris and the building's management company are being prosecuted for involuntary manslaughter.

The explosion that occurred in 2013 on Rue de Trévise in Paris resulted in the death of four people, the injury of fifty-three others, and more than a hundred additional victims. Eight years later, the Paris City Hall and the building's management company are on trial for involuntary manslaughter and involuntary injury before the Paris criminal court. This disaster was caused by an undetected explosion of natural gas in a building on Rue de Trévise, which caused the collapse of several floors and significant damage to neighboring buildings. The charges relate to the potential negligence of the parties in terms of building safety and maintenance, as well as the management of the crisis by the Paris City Hall after the explosion. The trial is eagerly awaited to determine whether the parties are responsible for the tragic consequences of this explosion.
