### Fake pilot, fake doctor, real prison: Frank Abagnale, the con artist who fooled America.


Title: Frank Abagnale, the con artist who fooled the United States

Frank Abagnale is a notorious con artist known for having committed multiple frauds in the United States in the 1960s. He defrauded thousands of dollars by impersonating a pilot, a doctor, and a lawyer, while also issuing fraudulent bank transfers.

At the age of 19, he had already been arrested more than a dozen times. In 1969, he was finally captured in Paris and extradited to the United States to be tried. He was sentenced to four years in prison and spent six years behind bars.

After his release, Frank Abagnale cooperated with the FBI, using his skills to combat bank fraud. He worked for the agency for more than 30 years and helped to bring down thousands of criminals.
