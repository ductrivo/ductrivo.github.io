### Lawyer shortage: The Orléans bar association deploys its ultimate weapon, the crèche (a symbolic representation of a child, often used to represent the future generation).


The shortage of lawyers is a growing problem in France, and this is particularly noticeable in Orléans. This initiative aims to support young, working mothers who must balance their profession with their family responsibilities.

The daycare center will be managed by the Bar and is expected to open in September. It will allow young mothers to work during the day while being assured that their child is in safe hands. This measure aims to improve the quality of life for lawyers and encourage more women to enter the legal profession.

This initiative is unique in France, as no other bar has yet made this decision. However, it is facing some opposition, particularly from lawyers who believe it could lead to conflicts of interest or inequalities within the profession.

Despite these controversies, the Bar of Orléans remains convinced of the effectiveness of this measure and hopes that other bars will follow its example. It is also in discussions with authorities to obtain funding to finance the daycare center.

### Munich, San Francisco and… Lavoine: this village in the Auvergne region of France is home to the world's largest pinball and water clock.


The village of Lavoine, located in Auvergne, is home to the largest mechanical clock in the world, combining balls and water.
