### Grenoble: The mutual clinic taken over by the Georges Boissel Berjallian Foundation


{% include figure.html
    src="https://www.ici.fr/pikapi/images/87ac2120-adbb-43dc-9710-252259e38804/920x517?webp=false"
    alt="Grenoble: The mutual clinic taken over by the Georges Boissel Berjallian Foundation"
    caption="Grenoble: The mutual clinic taken over by the Georges Boissel Berjallian Foundation" %}


The Grenoble mutual clinic was taken over by the Berjallienne Georges Boissel Foundation, following the rejection by the Grenoble Court of Justice of the continuity plan proposed by the AVEC group, which had taken over the clinic in 2020. The court's decision was based on financial and legal reasons, including the lack of sufficient guarantees to ensure the continuation of medical activities and the protection of patient interests. The Berjallienne Georges Boissel Foundation is a non-profit organization that has already taken over several healthcare facilities in the south-east of France, including the Saint-Étienne hospital and the Montélimar University Hospital Center.
