### Parking control in Grenoble: automated license plate reading, ticketing… Everything you need to know about the arrival of cars with LAPI


{% include figure.html
    src="https://france3-regions.franceinfo.fr/image/Ar0DsimgUAVKeuzYMCp3t9R1Dk8/930x620/filters:format(webp)/regions/2026/10/04/6ac215273dd23268852202.jpg"
    alt="Parking control in Grenoble: automated license plate reading, ticketing… Everything you need to know about the arrival of cars with LAPI"
    caption="Parking control in Grenoble: automated license plate reading, ticketing… Everything you need to know about the arrival of cars with LAPI" %}


Parking control in Grenoble is improved with the arrival of LAPI (Automatic License Plate Reading) vehicles. Starting Monday, two vehicles equipped with automatic cameras for reading license plates will circulate in the city to control paid parking.

The operation of this system involves taking a photo of the car's license plate, which will then be compared to a database to determine whether parking is authorized or not. If a check detects illegal parking, a notice will be sent by email or SMS to the car owner.

For drivers who want to pay for parking, it is possible to use automatic booths or mobile apps. The process for paying is simple and quick.

It is important to note that this system does not replace traditional parking control agents, but rather complements their work by allowing for more effective and rapid control. Drivers must therefore always comply with the current parking rules in Grenoble.

This system is a measure aimed at improving the management of paid parking in the city, by making control more effective and facilitating payments for drivers.

*[Source](https://france3-regions.franceinfo.fr/auvergne-rhone-alpes/isere/grenoble/controle-du-stationnement-a-grenoble-lecture-automatisee-des-plaques-verbalisation-ce-qu-il-faut-savoir-sur-l-arrivee-des-voitures-lapi-3427662.html)*

### Roc d'Azur 2026: program, participation, favorites... Everything you need to know about the 42nd edition this weekend.


The Roc d'Azur 2026 is the 42nd edition of this international motor racing competition, which takes place in Monte-Carlo, in the Principality of Monaco, from March 18th to 20th. This annual event attracts thousands of spectators and is considered one of the most prestigious motor racing events in the world.

The program includes several different races: the Formula 1 Grand Prix, the GT World Challenge Europe Powered by AWS, the Formula E Sprint Race, as well as karting and historic rally events. The favorites for victory are the most successful drivers in each category.

To participate in this edition, it is necessary to be registered with the organizing body. The number of places is limited and allocated based on criteria such as sporting achievements, race results, and the driver's reputation.

Spectators can watch the races from various vantage points, including in the official grandstands or outside the circuits. Special passes are available for access to privileged areas.
