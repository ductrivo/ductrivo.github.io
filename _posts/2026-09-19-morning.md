### Driving license, internships, first aid… Five new aids for young people in Île-de-France.


The five new measures announced for young people in the Île-de-France region aim to improve their professional and social integration.

1. Driving license: a program will allow young people without a license to take free courses to obtain their B license.
2. Internships: young people facing difficulties will be encouraged to undertake internships in local businesses, with a guaranteed minimum wage.
3. First aid: a training program in first aid will be implemented for young people who are unemployed.
4. Vocational training: vocational training workshops will be organized in priority areas, with an offer tailored to local needs.
5. Access to culture: a program will allow young people to benefit from a Culture Pass card to access cultural and sporting events free of charge throughout the Île-de-France region.

These measures aim to help young people facing difficulties acquire useful skills for their professional integration, to develop their autonomy, and to actively participate in the cultural life of their region.
