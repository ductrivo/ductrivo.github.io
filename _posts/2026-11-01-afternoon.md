### Gas prices, pensions, housing, anti-obesity treatments: what changes on November 1st


{% include figure.html
    src="https://www.ici.fr/pikapi/images/1729624f-6885-4361-b85c-6e7915a560cf/920x517?webp=false"
    alt="Gas prices, pensions, housing, anti-obesity treatments: what changes on November 1st"
    caption="Gas prices, pensions, housing, anti-obesity treatments: what changes on November 1st" %}


On November 1, 2026, several changes will come into effect in France. These affect gas prices, supplementary pensions, daily allowances, housing, obesity treatment, and automotive equipment. Here is a summary of the changes:

1. Gas prices: Domestic consumption tariffs increase by 5%.
2. Supplementary pensions: The age limit for accessing supplementary pensions is lowered to 60 years, and the mandatory contribution increases to 10% of gross salary.
3. Daily allowances: Daily allowances are increased by 5%.
4. Housing: The strengthened law on homeownership came into effect, with measures to facilitate the purchase of a home and a reduction in value-added tax (VAT) for social housing construction.
5. Obesity treatment: Medical treatments for obesity are now covered by social security, with an increase in consultation and treatment costs.
6. Automotive equipment: Environmental standards for CO2 emissions have become stricter, leading to an increase in the prices of vehicles that do not meet the new standards.

### Mushrooms and tree roots


{% include figure.html
    src="https://www.ici.fr/pikapi/images/87a9d2e9-c3e8-423b-894f-e9cfb5d203db/920x517?webp=false"
    alt="Mushrooms and tree roots"
    caption="Mushrooms and tree roots" %}


Mushrooms and tree roots have been linked for a long time. We have known about their relationship for many years, including the fact that certain types of mushrooms, such as truffles, only grow at the base of certain trees. However, there are also deadly relationships between certain fungi and plant roots.

This symbiotic relationship is called mycorrhization. The fungi provide plants with mineral nutrients that they cannot absorb directly from the soil, while the plants provide the fungi with carbohydrates produced by photosynthesis.

However, some parasitic fungi can develop on tree roots and infect them, which can lead to their death. Parasitic fungi are responsible for many tree diseases, such as white rot and leaf rust.

Mycorrhizal fungi can also help plants to resist environmental stresses, such as drought or pollution. They can also help plants to better absorb water and mineral nutrients from the soil.

In conclusion, mushrooms and tree roots are linked by a symbiotic relationship that can be beneficial for plants, but it can also lead to their death if parasitic fungi develop on their roots.

Supplementary pensions will see their rates increase by 2 points, reaching 7% for pensions over 3,000 euros per month and 5% for pensions below this amount.
