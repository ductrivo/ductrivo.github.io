### Why is the Grange des Chaumettes in Oris-en-Ratier, in Matheysine, unique in Isère?


{% include figure.html
    src="https://www.ici.fr/pikapi/images/d43e00a3-60b1-4716-ba4b-12a5cec837a9/920x517?webp=false"
    alt="Why is the Grange des Chaumettes in Oris-en-Ratier, in Matheysine, unique in Isère?"
    caption="Why is the Grange des Chaumettes in Oris-en-Ratier, in Matheysine, unique in Isère?" %}


The Grange des Chaumettes in Oris-en-Ratier, in the Matheysine (Isère), is unique due to its particularly distinctive architecture compared to traditional local barns. Built with dry stone, this remarkable structure has no equivalent in the region. Although other similar vaulted caves once existed on Otis-en-Ratier mountain, the Grange des Chaumettes is currently the only one of its kind that has been preserved. This construction dates back to the Middle Ages and is considered a remarkable example of medieval rural architecture in Isère.

*[Source](https://www.ici.fr/emissions/tout-s-explique-ici-isere/pourquoi-la-grange-des-chaumettes-d-oris-en-ratier-en-matheysine-est-elle-unique-en-isere-6341251?at_medium=lien_RSS&at_locale=29)*
