However, it has not been confirmed whether French authorities have received an adoption request or if they have begun to consider the possibility of adoption. Line Renaud is a French singer and actress known for her many successes in music and theater. Claude Chirac, on the other hand, was adopted by Jacques Chirac after her biological mother died in 1986.
