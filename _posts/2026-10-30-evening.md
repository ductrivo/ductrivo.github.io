### Russia reinstated in the Ice Hockey World Championships despite opposition from the International Federation


Russia was reinstated to participate in the Ice Hockey World Championships, despite the initial opposition from the International Ice Hockey Federation (IIHF). The country had been suspended in April 2017 due to political reasons related to the Russian invasion of Ukraine in 2014. However, after an extraordinary meeting of the IIHF executive committee on May 16, 2018, the suspension was lifted and Russia was able to participate in the World Championships, which were held in Denmark in May-June 2018. The decision was made after Russia agreed to pay a fine of 150,000 euros and to organize hockey matches against Ukraine to support humanitarian initiatives in the country.
