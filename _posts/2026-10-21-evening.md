### Cremation: What are the rules for scattering ashes?


{% include figure.html
    src="https://www.ici.fr/pikapi/images/d8f8403c-5e84-4b00-972f-4aa4206bdc7c/920x517?webp=false"
    alt="Cremation: What are the rules for scattering ashes?"
    caption="Cremation: What are the rules for scattering ashes?" %}


Cremation: What are the rules for scattering ashes?

In France, the right to scatter the ashes of your loved ones after cremation is permitted, but it is governed by the law of January 27, 2008, concerning the end of life and the rights of the elderly.

The ashes can be scattered in a public or private place belonging to the individual or their close relatives, provided that the place is respectful of the natural environment and is not visible from a public road. The scattering of ashes is prohibited in inland, coastal, or river waters, as well as on any land belonging to the public domain.

It is also possible to ask a cemetery to receive the ashes by creating a burial niche or burying the ashes in a family crypt. Cemeteries can also offer specific spaces for the scattering of ashes, subject to respecting local and regulatory rules.

It is important to consult the relevant authorities before any action of scattering ashes, in order to avoid any risk of criminal or administrative sanctions.
