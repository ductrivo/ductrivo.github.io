### Housing: The housing allowance (APL) will not be increased in 2027, according to the government.


{% include figure.html
    src="https://www.ici.fr/pikapi/images/b2d60f0c-9b3d-4fa1-98ab-f0dd255131a7/920x517?webp=false"
    alt="Housing: The housing allowance (APL) will not be increased in 2027, according to the government."
    caption="Housing: The housing allowance (APL) will not be increased in 2027, according to the government." %}


The government has announced that personalized housing allowances (APL) will not be reindexed to reflect inflation in 2027, meaning they will remain at their current level. This decision is part of the planned savings in next year's budget, announced by the Minister of City and Housing, Vincent Jeanbrun. This means that the amounts of the APL will not be increased as they have been every year to account for inflation.
