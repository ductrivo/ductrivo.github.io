### Booking.com scam: how we posted a false rental advertisement


Title: Fraud on Booking.com: How we published a fake rental advertisement

As part of an ethics in computer science exercise, a group of students attempted to highlight the vulnerability of the Booking.com hotel booking platform by publishing a fake rental advertisement.

To carry out this operation, the students created a user account on the Booking.com website and published a fake advertisement for an apartment that did not exist in Paris. The advertisement included photos of real apartments taken from the internet and detailed information about the location, equipment, and services offered.

The students then sent confirmation emails to several people who were interested in the fraudulent advertisement. They also created a website to welcome clients and provide them with additional information about the rental.

However, when the clients went to the location indicated in the advertisement, they were informed that the apartment did not exist and that they had been victims of fraud. The students were also contacted by Booking.com to investigate the publication of the fraudulent advertisement.

This exercise highlighted the vulnerability of the Booking.com platform to fraudulent advertisements and emphasized the importance of rigorous verification of the information published on the website. The students also learned that publishing a fraudulent advertisement can have serious consequences for clients and for their own reputation.
