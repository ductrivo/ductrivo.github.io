The government has announced an extension and expansion of aid for large vehicles, aimed at helping the most affected French people cope with rising fuel prices. Here's how to check your eligibility and apply:

1. Eligibility: Individuals and legal entities can benefit from this aid if they exceed certain fuel usage limits, such as purchasing more than 800 liters of gasoline or diesel per month for individuals, or using more than 15,000 liters of fuel per month for businesses.
2. Application: French people can apply for this aid online on the government website (service-public.fr) or by contacting their bank, insurance company, or energy supplier. They will need to provide documents such as their fuel bills and tax returns to prove their eligibility.
3. Amount: The aid can range from up to 200 euros per month for individuals and up to 1,500 euros per month for businesses, depending on their fuel consumption.
4. Duration: This aid will be available until December 31, 2022.
