In response to these accidents, manufacturers began to develop safer electric pruning shears. They added hand guards to protect the user's hands and improved the design of their blades to reduce the risk of injury.

Despite these efforts, accidents involving electric pruning shears continue to occur. Users must always be careful when using these tools and follow safety instructions to minimize the risk of injury.

In conclusion, machines such as the electric pruning shears can be useful but also dangerous. It is important to use them carefully and follow safety instructions to avoid accidents.
