### Lightning, heavy rain, mudslides, wind: what precautions should be taken during a hiking trip during a thunderstorm?


{% include figure.html
    src="https://www.ici.fr/pikapi/images/ff8d4907-596b-49f8-aeb0-19ddc153220e/920x517?webp=false"
    alt="Lightning, heavy rain, mudslides, wind: what precautions should be taken during a hiking trip during a thunderstorm?"
    caption="Lightning, heavy rain, mudslides, wind: what precautions should be taken during a hiking trip during a thunderstorm?" %}


When hiking, it is important to take certain precautions when a storm occurs to ensure safety. It is recommended to check the weather forecast and stay informed about weather changes. In the event of an imminent storm, it is important to take cover to avoid lightning strikes. This may involve moving to a higher location or finding a sheltered area. It is also recommended to avoid streams, as they can become more dangerous when it is raining. When camping, it is best to set up your tent with the door facing away from the wind to prevent it from being blown away. It is also important to protect yourself from the rain by wearing appropriate clothing and avoiding staying in the water or under trees for too long, as these can be struck by lightning. Finally, it is recommended not to use electrical equipment during a storm while hiking.

### Tomato size: a useful gesture, but not mandatory


{% include figure.html
    src="https://www.ici.fr/pikapi/images/5eefdf00-986d-4a6d-bc90-2bda9c230fd0/920x517?webp=false"
    alt="Tomato size: a useful gesture, but not mandatory"
    caption="Tomato size: a useful gesture, but not mandatory" %}


Pruning tomatoes is a useful but non-systematic practice, as it depends on the variety. Jean-Yves Meignen explains how to identify tomatoes that should be pruned and offers advice on how to encourage harvesting.

There are two types of tomatoes: those that should be pruned to promote their growth, and those that do not require pruning. Pruned tomatoes are typically larger, with a limited number of fruits per plant. They need to be pruned regularly to allow air and light to circulate around the fruits.

Tomatoes that do not require pruning are typically smaller, with a large number of fruits per plant. They can be left to grow freely without harming the harvest.

To encourage the harvest of pruned tomatoes, it is important to prune them regularly and not remove too many leaves. It is also important to ensure regular watering and to protect the plants from pests and diseases.

Finally, it is recommended to plant pruned and non-pruned tomatoes together to diversify the harvest and avoid potential problems with diseases or pests.
