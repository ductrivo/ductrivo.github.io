### How did the Matheysine arch, this natural arch between Pierre-Châtel and La Motte-d'Aveillans, form?


{% include figure.html
    src="https://www.ici.fr/pikapi/images/e610664f-c9cb-4e89-a96e-92d383e9381f/920x517?webp=false"
    alt="How did the Matheysine arch, this natural arch between Pierre-Châtel and La Motte-d'Aveillans, form?"
    caption="How did the Matheysine arch, this natural arch between Pierre-Châtel and La Motte-d'Aveillans, form?" %}


The "Pierced Stone" of Matheysine is a natural arch located between Pierre-Châtel and La Motte-d'Aveillans in the Dauphiné region. This emblem of the region, considered one of the wonders of the Dauphiné, can be easily reached thanks to several hiking trails. Its origin dates back to several hundred million years ago and is linked to the Matheysine coal. This natural formation was created by the erosion of underground waters, which carved a cavity in the limestone, forming a hole 12 meters high and 6 meters wide. Today, it is a popular tourist attraction for visitors interested in the nature and history of the region.

### In Odile's mind, who suffers from hemochromatosis, a disease treated 'like in the Middle Ages'


In Odile's brain, which is affected by hemochromatosis, a disease treated as in the Middle Ages, unfolds a story marked by health difficulties. Hemochromatosis is a rare genetic disease that causes an excess accumulation of iron in the human body. Odile suffers from this disease, which requires regular treatments to remove the excess iron from her body.

Odile's disease is so rare that it is compared to a disease of the Middle Ages, because it requires a treatment similar to that used during this historical period. Indeed, before the discovery of blood transfusions and modern treatment techniques, people with hemochromatosis were treated by regularly extracting blood to reduce the excess iron in their bodies.

Odile therefore has to undergo regular phlebotomies to maintain her health. This represents a significant burden for her, as each phlebotomy takes several hours and requires regular medical follow-up. Despite this, Odile bravely fights against her disease and tries to live a normal life.

She is also involved in awareness activities for hemochromatosis, as she wants more people to be informed about this rare disease and that it can be diagnosed earlier. She hopes that people affected can benefit from more effective and less painful treatments.

Finally, Odile is a strong and courageous person who does not let her disease defeat her. She continues to live her life with optimism and seeks solutions to improve her health and that of other people affected by rare diseases.
