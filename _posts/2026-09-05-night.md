### Fires: Why are firefighters going on strike starting Tuesday?

    alt="Fires: Why are firefighters going on strike starting Tuesday?"
    caption="Fires: Why are firefighters going on strike starting Tuesday?" %}


French firefighters are starting a strike from Tuesday, September 19th, until October 20th. This decision is due to the recurring summer fires, which have significantly damaged their resources and morale. The firefighters are demanding more material resources and massive recruitment to deal with these difficult situations. They also emphasize that their work is physically demanding and that they are often exposed to risks. This strike could have significant consequences for the safety of citizens in the event of a fire, and authorities have called for rapid negotiations to find a solution acceptable to all parties.
