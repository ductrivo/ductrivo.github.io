    caption="Drought: 61% of groundwater levels are below normal" %}


The drought persists in France: According to data published by the BRGM on September 8, 61% of groundwater reserves are below their normal level and 90% are declining. This situation is due to a summer characterized by a lack of rainfall. This could have significant consequences for water supply, agriculture, and the environment. It is therefore necessary to continue monitoring the situation and to adopt appropriate measures to reduce the negative effects of this drought.

### 'Black Market' for Cigarettes: The Shocking Statistic Ordered by the Tobacco Industry


The black market for cigarettes is a controversial topic, studied in a report commissioned by the tobacco industry. This illegal market involves cigarettes sold without taxes or duties, which allows sellers to profit from lower prices and consumers to pay less.

According to the report's figures, the black market represents approximately 10% of cigarettes sold in the European Union, or nearly 25 billion cigarettes per year. The countries most affected are France, Germany, and Italy.

The report highlights that the black market is a threat to public health due to the health risks associated with the consumption of illegal cigarettes. These cigarettes can contain more toxic substances and have fewer quality guarantees than legal cigarettes.

Furthermore, the black market results in significant losses for public treasuries, as cigarette taxes are not paid. According to the report's estimates, the losses for the European Union amount to approximately 10 billion euros per year.

To reduce the black market for cigarettes, it is necessary to take concrete measures. The report proposes, among other things, to strengthen border controls and to implement sanctions for sellers of illegal cigarettes. It is also important to educate consumers about the health risks associated with the consumption of illegal cigarettes.

In conclusion, the black market for cigarettes is a serious problem for public health and public finances in Europe. Therefore, it is necessary to take concrete measures to reduce its impact.
