### foiled attack on the Bank of America in Paris: how the FBI links Iran to teenagers from Montreuil


FBI thwarted an attack against the Bank of America in Paris: how the FBI links Iran to teenagers from Montreuil

The FBI has revealed that teenagers from Montreuil, France, were involved in a plot to carry out an attack against the Bank of America in Paris. According to information available, Iran would be involved in this plot.

The teenagers would have received instructions and funding from Iran to carry out their mission. They would have been trained in the handling of explosives and equipped with equipment to carry out the attack.

The FBI discovered the links between the teenagers and Iran by analyzing intercepted electronic communications. The messages indicated that the teenagers had received instructions from Iran to carry out their mission.

French authorities have arrested several people linked to this plot, including some members of Hezbollah, an Iranian-backed armed group. The authorities have also seized explosives and other equipment used to carry out the attack.

The French Ministry of the Interior has stated that this plot is linked to Iran's foreign policy, which seeks to destabilize Western countries. The authorities have also emphasized that this case highlights the need for international cooperation to combat terrorism.

The FBI has said that it will continue to work with French authorities to investigate this plot and identify all individuals involved. The authorities have also called on citizens to remain vigilant and report any suspicious behavior.
