### Parcoursup, number of students per class: government measures to respond to the anger of high school and university students


{% include figure.html
    src="https://www.ici.fr/pikapi/images/be67480d-d41c-4640-9e5c-778d52af3648/920x517?webp=false"
    alt="Parcoursup, number of students per class: government measures to respond to the anger of high school and university students"
    caption="Parcoursup, number of students per class: government measures to respond to the anger of high school and university students" %}


The French government has announced measures aimed at responding to the protests by high school and university students, which have been ongoing for several weeks. Prime Minister Sébastien Lecornu met with student and high school organizations on Monday to present these measures.

Among them, the Parcoursup platform will be eliminated in 2028. This platform allowed students to choose their classes and universities in France. It has been criticized for its complex and inefficient operation.

The measures announced by the government also aim to reduce the number of students per class, which should improve the quality of teaching. In addition, students will be allowed to take online courses to supplement their training, even though this is not mandatory.

The government has also announced that it will invest in the continuing education of teachers and in improving school equipment. It has also promised to increase the number of places available for vocational students, who are often victims of competition to find a place in their preferred university.

Finally, the government has announced that it will create an advisory committee composed of students and teachers to discuss the problems faced by high schools and students. This committee should allow students to actively participate in decision-making regarding their education.

Student and high school organizations have praised these measures, but have also asked the government to do more to respond to the demands of students, particularly in terms of school fees and housing.
