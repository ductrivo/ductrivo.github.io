### Why were the houses in Pont-en-Royans built on the edge of the cliff?


{% include figure.html
    src="https://www.ici.fr/pikapi/images/2dc7a5da-656b-4590-8578-5f5c93f13155/920x517?webp=false"
    alt="Why were the houses in Pont-en-Royans built on the edge of the cliff?"
    caption="Why were the houses in Pont-en-Royans built on the edge of the cliff?" %}


The houses in Pont-en-Royans were built on the cliff edge for historical and practical reasons. Located in the Massif Central in France, they are characterized by their unique architecture, built on the rocks.

The site has been inhabited since Roman times, but the construction of these houses dates back to the Middle Ages. At that time, the village was an important trading crossroads between the Rhône Valley and the neighboring mountain regions. The position on the cliff allowed for a panoramic view of the valley and control of trade routes.

The houses were built using the rocks as support, which reduced the cost of construction and minimized the impact on the environment. The walls are made of dry stone, without mortar, and the roofs are covered with thatch.

Today, these houses are a popular tourist destination, known for their unique architecture and beauty. They were listed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 2008. Despite their popularity, the houses are still inhabited by local families.

In conclusion, the houses of Pont-en-Royans were built on the cliff edge for historical and practical reasons, including controlling trade routes and reducing the cost of construction. They are now a popular tourist destination and a unique example of traditional French architecture.
