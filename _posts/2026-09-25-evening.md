### Sterilization, CO2 traps: what are the truly effective techniques for combating the tiger mosquito?


{% include figure.html
    src="https://www.ici.fr/pikapi/images/6ae6a542-c8f6-40bc-bd88-2a4e1b4724ae/920x517?webp=false"
    alt="Sterilization, CO2 traps: what are the truly effective techniques for combating the tiger mosquito?"
    caption="Sterilization, CO2 traps: what are the truly effective techniques for combating the tiger mosquito?" %}


Effective techniques for combating the tiger mosquito are diverse.

Regular cleaning of damp areas around the house, such as stagnant water pools or drainage channels, can reduce their population by preventing their reproduction.

CO2 traps are also used to attract and trap mosquitoes. They work by releasing carbon dioxide, which mimics human respiration and attracts mosquitoes to a light source where they can be trapped.

Mosquito sterilization is also an effective method for reducing their population. The females are captured, sterilized with gamma radiation, and released back into the wild. They can no longer reproduce and transmit the diseases they carry.

Finally, the use of citronella or eucalyptus-based repellents can also help protect people from mosquitoes. It is important to apply them regularly to maintain their effectiveness.

In Gironde, where the tiger mosquito transmits Chikungunya, these techniques can be useful for protecting the population from this threat.

### Sending French military equipment to Saudi Arabia: why is the Yanbu oil terminal so important?


The Yanbu oil terminal, located in Saudi Arabia, is a key point in the global oil industry. It is used for the processing and storage of crude oil, petrochemical products, and liquefied natural gas (LNG). With a capacity of 4 million barrels per day, it represents a quarter of the country's total liquid oil production.

The terminal is also important due to its strategic location. It is located on the west coast of Saudi Arabia, near the Red Sea and the Mediterranean Sea. This allows for easy export to European, Asian, and African markets.

Due to its economic and strategic importance, the Yanbu terminal is often targeted by terrorist or military attacks. In 2017, it was the target of a drone attack that caused significant damage.
