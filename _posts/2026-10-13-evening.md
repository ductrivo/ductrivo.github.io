### Why do insects fly around the lights at night?


{% include figure.html
    src="https://www.ici.fr/pikapi/images/331f329a-8d91-45d8-a947-38bfa6076662/920x517?webp=false"
    alt="Why do insects fly around the lights at night?"
    caption="Why do insects fly around the lights at night?" %}


Insects fly around the lamps at night due to a light effect called the sector effect. This effect occurs when the light is concentrated in a narrow angle, which confuses the insects about their direction and position. This causes them to fly around the lamps at night.

Night lighting can have harmful effects on insects, birds, and biodiversity in general. Insects are attracted to light and can easily be captured by predators or hit by windows. Birds can also be affected by night lighting, as it can disrupt their ability to hunt effectively and their breeding cycle.

The Regional Natural Park of the Monts d'Ardèche emphasizes the importance of reducing night lighting to protect biodiversity. Measures that can be taken include using low-intensity lights, directing lights downwards, and using specific light filters to reduce the harmful effects on insects and birds.

*[Source](https://www.ici.fr/emissions/mais-pourquoi-daphne/pourquoi-les-insectes-tournent-ils-autour-des-lampes-la-nuit-2251454?at_medium=lien_RSS&at_locale=24)*
