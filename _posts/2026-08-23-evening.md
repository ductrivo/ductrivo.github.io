### Chronic fatigue syndrome officially recognized as a disease


Chronic Fatigue Syndrome (CFS) is a recognized disease by medical authorities. It is a persistent and disproportionate fatigue in relation to physical or mental exertion, which can last for several months or even years. Symptoms include muscle pain, headaches, general weakness, sleep disturbances, as well as cognitive problems such as difficulty concentrating or remembering. CFS can be caused by various factors, including viral infections, physical trauma, medications, and psychiatric conditions. It is important to identify an underlying cause for appropriate treatment. Patients can also benefit from multidisciplinary care, including psychological, physical, and pharmacological treatment.

### 'Not competitive': why the 'Arctic Silk Road' is still a mirage


The "Arctic Silk Road" project remains a long-term goal rather than an imminent reality, as it faces several technical and economic obstacles.

The main difficulty lies in the extreme Arctic climate, where ice and cold make navigation and the construction of roads difficult. Ships must navigate through shallow, icy waters, which requires significant investment in specialized maritime infrastructure.

Furthermore, the high cost of building and maintaining such a road would make it economically unviable. The countries involved would also need to agree on navigation and trade rules to avoid diplomatic conflicts.

In addition, the Arctic Silk Road would not be a direct alternative to the original Silk Road, which connected Europe to China by land. The distance between Arctic ports and those in East Asia is too great to allow for efficient goods transport.

Finally, there are environmental concerns related to the Arctic Silk Road, particularly regarding pollution and climate change. Human activities in the Arctic could have a negative impact on the region's fragile ecosystems.

In conclusion, the realization of the Arctic Silk Road is still far from being a reality, as it faces technical, economic, and environmental obstacles. The countries involved should continue to work together to overcome these difficulties and make this project viable.
