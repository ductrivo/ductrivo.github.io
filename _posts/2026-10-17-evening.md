### 'Buried like dogs': In the Parisian cemetery of Thiais, a memorial is being demanded for the Algerians massacred on October 17, 1961.


The "Buried Like Dogs" memorial is requested in the Parisian cemetery of Thiais as a tribute to the Algerians massacred on October 17, 1961, in Paris. This peaceful demonstration was brutally suppressed by the French police, resulting in the deaths of over 200 people and hundreds more injured. The victims were buried in this cemetery without any distinguishing markers, and their resting place remains unknown to many of their families. The proposed memorial is intended to honor the memory of these people and to recall the tragic history of this era. It will include a monument with the names of known victims, as well as a resting area for the remains of unidentified victims. The project is supported by several associations and organizations, including some that represent the families of the victims.
