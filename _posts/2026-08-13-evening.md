### At a height of 50 cm from the ground with strollers: 36 inexperienced hikers rescued in the Austrian Alps


In the Austrian Alps, sixty-two people were rescued by rescuers after being found 50 centimeters from the cliff edge with their babies in strollers. The inexperienced hikers had gotten lost and were in danger. The rescuers were able to locate them using a GPS device and rescued them without any injuries. This event highlighted the importance of safety when hiking in the mountains, as well as the crucial role of rescuers.
