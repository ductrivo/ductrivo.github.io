### Olivier Menut, Chairman, CEO, and Founder of Orikio, 'ear augmentation specialists'


Olivier Menut is the president, CEO, and founder of Orikio, a company known as "enhanced ears." Based in Isère, Orikio is a pioneering European company in acoustic monitoring using artificial intelligence (AI). Founded in 2016, the company has developed innovative technologies to analyze and interpret sound data in real time. These technologies are used in various fields such as public safety, industry, construction, and healthcare. Orikio has also developed an acoustic monitoring platform for cities, which allows for real-time monitoring of noise levels in streets and thus improves urban quality of life. The company's clients include local governments, businesses, and individuals.

### Inclusive activities offered by 'Tandem Loisirs'


{% include figure.html
    src="https://www.ici.fr/pikapi/images/ee30ff19-7846-4a06-80e0-fe926f422261/920x517?webp=false"
    alt="Inclusive activities offered by 'Tandem Loisirs'"
    caption="Inclusive activities offered by 'Tandem Loisirs'" %}


The Tandem Leisure association, based in Romans-sur-Isère for thirteen years, offers inclusive activities for people with motor or sensory disabilities. Members of the association can cycle in tandems and go for walks. Pierre Sanial, one of the volunteer guides for this human adventure, talks to us about their mission.

The goal of Tandem Leisure is to offer a sporting experience accessible to everyone, regardless of their disability or physical level. The activities offered are adapted to the individual needs of the participants and allow them to develop their autonomy and self-confidence.

The tandems are equipped with a steering system that allows a non-disabled driver to control the direction of the bike, while the passenger, with a motor or sensory disability, can pedal at their own pace. The walks organized by the association are also adapted to the needs of the participants, with easy or difficult routes depending on their level of mobility.

Pierre Sanial has been one of the volunteer guides for Tandem Leisure for several years. He explains that his passion for his work comes from seeing the participants develop and improve thanks to the activities offered by the association. He also emphasizes the important role of volunteers in carrying out this mission, particularly during events organized by Tandem Leisure, such as hiking or cycling trips.

In conclusion, Tandem Leisure is an association that offers inclusive sporting activities for people with motor or sensory disabilities. The tandems and walks organized by the association allow participants to develop their autonomy and self-confidence, thanks to the active participation of volunteers in carrying out this mission.
