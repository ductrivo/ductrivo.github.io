### What were the protruding elements visible on the facades of some houses in Mens used for?


{% include figure.html
    src="https://www.ici.fr/pikapi/images/cc1e1b1f-7938-47dc-9884-de34b6f8eb88/920x517?webp=false"
    alt="What were the protruding elements visible on the facades of some houses in Mens used for?"
    caption="What were the protruding elements visible on the facades of some houses in Mens used for?" %}


The "engrangeaux," these curious architectural elements visible on the facades of some buildings in Mens, were historically used to store hay and other agricultural materials. Located in the Trièves region, this commune was known as the "capital" of this territory. The engrangeaux are wooden or stone structures, often located above the entrances to houses, which allowed residents to store hay safely from the weather. Today, these architectural curiosities are protected and testify to a rich and diverse rural past. They are an important part of the local cultural heritage of Mens and are a tourist attraction for visitors interested in the history and traditions of this region.
