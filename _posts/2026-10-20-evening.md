### Following the violence in Paris, a bill of several million euros: the dizzying cost of a weekend of chaos


Following the riots in Paris over the weekend of October 1-3, 2020, the city suffered significant damage and incurred substantial costs. According to preliminary estimates, the material damage amounts to several million euros. Luxury stores, fashion boutiques, and restaurants were particularly affected by the violence. Hundreds of vehicles were also set on fire or damaged.

Authorities deployed additional forces to restore order, including the national police and gendarmes. The demonstrations caused hours of road and public transport blockages, as well as disruptions to postal services and delays for emergency calls.

The riots also caused damage to historic monuments in the city, including the Arc de Triomphe and the Louvre. Authorities stated that the damage to these sites would be repaired as quickly as possible to preserve their integrity.

The riots were triggered by rumors on social media that a police officer had killed a young black man, but investigators denied these allegations and stated that the young man had died in a car accident. Authorities called for calm and promised a thorough investigation into the events. They also announced that measures would be taken to prevent such riots in the future.

In this new installment, Shaun the Sheep and his friends return with an animated film that is full of charm and laughter. The story takes place around Halloween, where the farm animals decide to organize their own event.

Shaun, the leader of the sheep, is once again at the center of the action with his friends Bobert, Shirley, and Timmy. They must overcome a series of obstacles to organize the best possible Halloween party. The farm animals are helped by new characters such as witches, ghosts, and vampires.

The film is full of references to popular culture and horror films, which adds a humorous touch to the story. The animation is well-done, and the dialogue is funny and entertaining.

In addition to the comedy, the film also explores themes such as friendship, cooperation, and teamwork. Shaun the Sheep: Halloween Beast is an animated film that offers a fun experience for children and adults.
