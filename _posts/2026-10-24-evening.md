### Romans-sur-Isère. Former general hospital: as a refuge, a place of work, and a tool for discipline, Louis XV's pass for the poor.


{% include figure.html
    src="https://cdn-s-www.ledauphine.com/images/074A8A0F-36F2-4BBC-A01D-99D4D83C3A19/NW_listB/ancien-hopital-general-refuge-lieu-de-travail-et-instrument-de-discipline-le-passeport-de-louis-xv-aux-pauvres-1792849818.jpg"
    alt="Romans-sur-Isère. Former general hospital: as a refuge, a place of work, and a tool for discipline, Louis XV's pass for the poor."
    caption="Romans-sur-Isère. Former general hospital: as a refuge, a place of work, and a tool for discipline, Louis XV's pass for the poor." %}


The item presented is an exceptionally rare poster dating from 1736, reproducing the patent letters of Louis XV authorizing the creation of the General Hospital of Romans-sur-Isère. This institution, the result of half a century of efforts and changes, served as both a refuge, a place of work, and a means of discipline for the poor. Under Louis XV, the General Hospital of Romans-sur-Isère was a passport to social and medical assistance for the most destitute. Today, this historical object testifies to the king's and local authorities' desire to improve the condition of the poor by creating an institution that marked the history of Romans-sur-Isère.
