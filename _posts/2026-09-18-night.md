### European Heritage Days: 6 sites to discover in Isère this weekend


{% include figure.html
    src="https://www.ici.fr/pikapi/images/88daab62-5a0d-497e-b6ac-b189fb64c1cd/920x517?webp=false"
    alt="European Heritage Days: 6 sites to discover in Isère this weekend"
    caption="European Heritage Days: 6 sites to discover in Isère this weekend" %}


European Heritage Days will take place on Saturday, September 19 and Sunday, September 20, 2026, across Europe. In the Isère department, several historical sites will be exceptionally open to the public this weekend. Here are six sites to discover:

1. The Château de la Tour du Pin, a magnificent medieval castle located in the commune of La Tour-du-Pin.
2. The Abbey of Saint-André-le-Bas, a Romanesque abbey located in Grenoble, which houses remarkable frescoes from the 13th century.
3. The Musée Dauphinois, located in the Château de Saint-Georges in Grenoble, which presents the history and culture of the Dauphiné region.
4. The birthplace of Victor Hugo in Vic-sur-Aisne, where the famous writer was born in 1802.
5. The Château de Montfalcon, a medieval castle located in the commune of Saint-Marcel-lès-Valence, which houses local art and history collections.
6. The ancient city of Vienne, located near Valence, which presents the remains of a Roman city founded in 43 BC.

European Heritage Days offer a unique opportunity to discover and appreciate the history and culture of Isère. The sites will be open to visitors for free, but some activities may require advance booking.
