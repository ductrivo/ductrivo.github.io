Histo Bus quickly gained a reputation for its quality of service and social commitment. The company developed school, tourist, and urban transport services, as well as specialized services such as transport for people with disabilities.

In 2016, Histo Bus merged with Keolis, a French public transport company, to form Keolis Isère. Jean-Marie Guétat continued to lead the company until 2019, when he retired.

Throughout his career, Jean-Marie Guétat was recognized for his commitment to sustainable development and his involvement in social initiatives. He was also honored for his contributions to the public transport sector, including receiving the Silver Medal for Tourism Merit in 2015.

Today, Histo Bus is a major public transport company in the Grenoble region and continues to offer high-quality services to passengers.
