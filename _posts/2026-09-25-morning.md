He also spoke about the situation in Lebanon, where he called for political reforms to address the country's economic problems.

He called for a coalition of pro-European parties to win the elections and to advance Europe in terms of innovation, security, and human rights protection.

In conclusion, Emmanuel Macron's interview primarily addressed the issues of rising fuel prices and the Russian threat, but he also discussed several other issues related to his foreign policy and the European elections.

### Ski: France returns to being the world's top destination


{% include figure.html
    src="https://www.ici.fr/pikapi/images/39ae9d4c-f2f6-4f98-8e02-9d409fa83ba7/920x517?webp=false"
    alt="Ski: France returns to being the world's top destination"
    caption="Ski: France returns to being the world's top destination" %}


Skiing in France is once again the world's leading destination after eleven years without this status. This announcement was made by Yves Dimier, Vice-President of the French Ski Areas, during an interview on ICI Pays de Savoie on September 24th. According to official figures, France welcomed over 10 million skiers in 2020-2021, representing an increase of 35% compared to the previous season. This increase is due to several factors, including the increase in the number of ski areas and the development of alpine skiing infrastructure in France. Furthermore, the health measures taken to combat the Covid-19 pandemic allowed the resorts to remain open throughout the season, which contributed to attracting more visitors. Foreign skiers also represent a significant portion of visitors, with nearly 2 million tourists from abroad coming to France to ski this season. This shows that France is back on the international ski scene and offers excellent potential for winter sports resorts.
