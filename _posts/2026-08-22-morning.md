### VIDEOS - Cycling: Reflection on the career of Julian Alaphilippe, double world champion who is retiring


{% include figure.html
    src="https://www.ici.fr/pikapi/images/f273022f-2bf9-40c5-9f78-59b381e781a1/920x517?webp=false"
    alt="VIDEOS - Cycling: Reflection on the career of Julian Alaphilippe, double world champion who is retiring"
    caption="VIDEOS - Cycling: Reflection on the career of Julian Alaphilippe, double world champion who is retiring" %}


Julian Alaphilippe, a French cyclist of Aurverne origin, announced his retirement on Friday, January 28, at the age of 34. Alaphilippe, a double world champion, has marked French cycling history with his remarkable performances and aggressive racing style.

Born in 1988 in Saint-Amand-Montrond in the Cher, Alaphilippe began his professional career in 2014 with the French Europcar team. He won his first world title in 2018 during the road race at the Innsbruck World Championships in Austria. The following year, he repeated his victory in the same event in Yorkshire, UK.

Alaphilippe also won many prestigious races such as the Tour de France (2019), the Flèche Wallonne (2014, 2017 and 2018) and Liège-Bastogne-Liège (2019). He also finished second in the general classification of the Tour de France in 2019.

Alaphilippe was a popular cyclist in France, known for his aggressive racing style and his ability to win on the most difficult races. He was also recognized for his social commitment, particularly in supporting the fight against AIDS and participating in charitable activities.

Alaphilippe announced his retirement after finishing 12th in the 2022 Tour de France. He said that he wanted to retire to spend more time with his family and to pursue other projects.

His retirement announcement was welcomed by fans and French cycling professionals, who highlighted his important contribution to the sport. Alaphilippe will certainly leave a lasting mark in the history of French cycling.

### Ukraine War: What is the Pelikan radar, which detects drones and missiles at 400 km?


Ukraine War: What is the Pelican radar? The Pelican radar is a drone and missile detection system developed by Israel Aerospace Industries (IAI). It is capable of detecting targets at a range of 400 kilometers. This radar uses active electronically scanned array (AESA) technology to provide continuous and precise surveillance coverage, even in adverse weather conditions. The Pelican radar is designed to simultaneously detect and track multiple moving targets, including drones and ballistic missiles. It is also equipped with a threat neutralization function, which allows identified targets to be destroyed. The Pelican radar was first used in the conflict in Ukraine, where it was installed on Israeli-supplied armored vehicles provided to Ukraine by the Elbit Systems company.
