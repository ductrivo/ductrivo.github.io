Louis Fournier is the creator of La Galoche, a unique establishment in France known for its historical production of galoches (a type of shoe). The La Galoche industrial site is located in Saint-Malo-sur-Rance in the Manche, where Fournier began producing these shoes in 1894. Galoches are characterized by their horse-shoe shape and wooden soles, making them ideal for the marshy conditions of coastal areas.
