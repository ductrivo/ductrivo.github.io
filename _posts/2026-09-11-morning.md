### Squashes in late summer: watering, harvesting, and storage – the best practices


{% include figure.html
    src="https://www.ici.fr/pikapi/images/83e67938-5557-4a47-a7bf-9a33e54fd49d/920x517?webp=false"
    alt="Squashes in late summer: watering, harvesting, and storage – the best practices"
    caption="Squashes in late summer: watering, harvesting, and storage – the best practices" %}


At the end of summer in the Drôme-Ardèche region, pumpkins continue to mature in the garden. Jean-Yves Meignen offers his advice for September to promote the already formed fruits, knowing when to harvest and avoid storage errors.

To properly water pumpkins, it is important to avoid over-wetting the soil around the plants, as this can cause diseases. It is best to use a fine-jet tap and not to water directly on the leaves.

The harvest of pumpkins should be done when the fruit has reached its maximum size, but before the skin becomes too hard. It is important to cut the pumpkins above the stem to allow the plant to continue producing new fruits.

To store pumpkins, it is best to leave them to dry in a dry and well-ventilated place before removing the skin. The pumpkins can then be stored by placing them in airtight plastic bags or by freezing them.

It is important not to leave the pumpkins on the ground for too long before harvesting, as this can cause them to deteriorate and attract harmful insects. By following these tips, it is possible to obtain a plentiful and high-quality pumpkin harvest at the end of summer.
