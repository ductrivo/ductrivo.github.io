### Shooting at the Aubagne college: the 'True Crime Community' movement 'remains marginal,' according to the national anti-terrorism prosecutor.


On March 16, 2023, an attack took place at the Aubagne school in southern France. A man armed with a weapon killed a teacher and injured two others before being shot by the police.

The attack was claimed by a True Crime community, a group that glorifies violent crimes online. However, the national anti-terrorism prosecutor stated that this movement remains marginal and is not considered a major terrorist threat.

The French police opened an investigation to determine the motives for the attack and whether other people were involved. The authorities also implemented additional security measures in schools across the country.

The French Minister of Education stated that the safety of schools is a priority and that an emergency meeting will be held with authorities to discuss measures to ensure the safety of students.

Parents of students were informed of the attack and were asked to come and pick up their children if they were absent from school. Schools also implemented additional security measures to protect students.

The attack provoked a strong reaction in the country, with demonstrations of support for the victims and calls for vigilance. The authorities also called on citizens to stay informed and not to spread false information on social media.
