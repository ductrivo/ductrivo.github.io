### 'Without the Olympics, there is no future,' the Nordic combined team expresses concern after the IOC's decision.


{% include figure.html
    src="https://www.ici.fr/pikapi/images/a335968e-52cc-4b6c-8337-d82688449783/920x517?webp=false"
    alt="'Without the Olympics, there is no future,' the Nordic combined team expresses concern after the IOC's decision."
    caption="'Sans JO, il n'y a pas d'avenir' le combiné nordique inquiet après la décision du CIO" %}


The Nordic combined team is worried after the International Olympic Committee's (IOC) decision to exclude this discipline from the 2030 Olympic Games. Indeed, Nordic combined, one of the oldest skiing disciplines, is not included in the Olympic program for the next editions. This decision puts its future at risk.

The report was made in Autrans, in Isère, at the foot of the jumps that hosted the events during the 1968 Winter Olympics in Grenoble. Nordic combined practitioners and coaches are concerned about their future, as the discipline no longer benefits from the media and financial exposure that the Olympic Games offer.

Nordic combined has been an Olympic discipline since the first Winter Games in 1924 in Chamonix. It consists of combining ski jumping and cross-country skiing, which makes it unique in the world of winter sports. Despite its history and popularity in Europe, it is often forgotten by the media and international competition organizers.

Nordic combined practitioners hope that this decision by the IOC will not be final and that they will be able to continue to participate in the Olympic Games in the future. They have also called on French sports authorities to take measures to support the discipline and give it greater visibility.

### Why do firefighters deliberately set fires?


Firefighters intentionally set fires as part of controlled burning operations, also known as forest clearing or forest management operations. This practice aims to reduce the risk of forest fires by eliminating accumulated combustible materials on the ground and in the canopy, as well as to prevent the spread of existing fires. Controlled burning operations are planned and carried out under favorable weather conditions to minimize negative effects on human health, the environment, and property. Firefighters follow strict protocols to ensure that the fires are controlled and do not degenerate into unwanted fires. In addition, controlled burning operations can help to maintain biodiversity by simulating natural events such as forest fires.

### Death of Nirmal Purja: Broad Peak ascent, expedition, victims… what we know about the fatal avalanche involving the star mountaineer


On July 5, 2021, Nirmal Purja, a Nepalese mountaineer known as "Nimsdai," died in an avalanche on the Broad Peak in the Pakistani Himalayas. He was leading an expedition aimed at summiting the 14 highest peaks in the world in 7 months, a project known as "Project Possible."

The expedition had already successfully summited several peaks, including K2 and Lhotse. However, during the ascent of Broad Peak, an avalanche occurred, killing Purja, along with two other members of the team, John Snorri and Tashi Sherpa.

Purja was considered an exceptional mountaineer, having already summited the 8 highest peaks in the world in under a year in 2019. He had also been awarded the Order of the Brave Nepalese for his outstanding achievements in mountaineering.

The accident caused shock and grief in the international mountaineering community, with expressions of admiration and condolences coming from all over the world. The Pakistani authorities announced that an investigation would be opened to determine the circumstances of the accident.

The expedition was suspended after the deaths of Purja and his colleagues, but it is possible that some members will continue to attempt to reach the remaining peaks in the "Project Possible" project.
