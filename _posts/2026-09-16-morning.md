    caption="'This is my second family': In Grenoble, the association 1CabasPour1Etudiant helps isolated students." %}


The 1CabasPour1Etudiant association provides support to isolated students in Grenoble. Founded a year ago, it helps young students facing financial or social difficulties. Lina, an Algerian student at the University of Grenoble, arrived in France alone. She turned to this association to find support and guidance. Thanks to their help, she was able to overcome the obstacles she faced and continue her studies.
