### François Berger, who came up with the idea of installing animal feeding bowls near the water fountains in Grenoble


{% include figure.html
    src="https://www.ici.fr/pikapi/images/dbcf1c67-61df-4ea0-a16d-81a5d0791f23/920x517?webp=false"
    alt="François Berger, who came up with the idea of installing animal feeding bowls near the water fountains in Grenoble"
    caption="François Berger, who came up with the idea of installing animal feeding bowls near the water fountains in Grenoble" %}


François Berger is a resident of Grenoble who proposed in 2018 to install animal water fountains near public fountains in the city. The idea was to allow dogs and other domestic animals to cool off during hot periods, while also maintaining public hygiene.

The project was supported by the Grenoble city council, and work began in 2019. Today, there are more than 30 animal water fountains installed in different neighborhoods of the city. The fountains are equipped with separate valves for animals and humans, which allows for the maintenance of public hygiene while also providing a practical solution for pet owners.

The project has been a popular success and has received praise from residents of Grenoble. It has also attracted the attention of national and international media, which have highlighted the innovative nature of the project.

François Berger was recognized for his initiative in 2019 by the Grenoble city council, which awarded him a special prize for citizen innovation. The project has also inspired other French cities to install animal water fountains near their public fountains.
