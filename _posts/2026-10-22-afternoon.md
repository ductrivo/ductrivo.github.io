### Sale of US weapons to Taiwan: Why is Donald Trump delaying approval of the €14 billion order?


Sale of US weapons to Taiwan: Why Donald Trump is delaying the approval of €14 billion in orders

The sale of weapons to Taiwan by the United States has been a subject of political debate for several years. In September 2020, it was reported that Donald Trump was delaying the approval of €14 billion in weapons orders for Taiwan.

The reasons cited for this delay are multiple. Firstly, the United States has official diplomatic relations with China since 1979 and recognizes Beijing as the sole representative of China at the UN. Therefore, the sale of weapons to Taiwan can be seen as a violation of this policy.

Secondly, the United States also has commercial interests in China and does not want to risk damaging its economic relations with the country. Finally, Donald Trump has also been criticized for his ambiguous stance towards Taiwan, particularly when he refused to participate in the inauguration ceremony of Taiwanese President Tsai Ing-wen in 2017.

Despite these obstacles, the United States has continued to sell weapons to Taiwan in the past, and it is possible that Donald Trump will ultimately approve the aforementioned orders. However, so far, no final decision has been made on this matter.
