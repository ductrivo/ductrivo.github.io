### Why would Champollion never have deciphered the hieroglyphs without the help of his brother?


{% include figure.html
    src="https://www.ici.fr/pikapi/images/98b5a0a5-6d6d-447e-b7ab-db80173729f2/920x517?webp=false"
    alt="Why would Champollion never have deciphered the hieroglyphs without the help of his brother?"
    caption="Why would Champollion never have deciphered the hieroglyphs without the help of his brother?" %}


Jacques-Joseph Champollion, who was twelve years older than his brother Jean-François, played a decisive role in his career and research. Indeed, Jacques-Joseph had a significant influence on the life and work of Jean-François in several areas.

First, Jacques-Joseph encouraged his brother to study Egyptian languages and hieroglyphs. In 1802, he sent Jean-François a copy of Silvestre de Sacy's book on Arabic grammar, which inspired Jean-François to conduct his own research.

Second, Jacques-Joseph helped his brother obtain a scholarship to study at the École des Ponts et Chaussées in 1804. This allowed him to train in the sciences and mathematics, which were useful for deciphering the hieroglyphs.

Finally, Jacques-Joseph financially supported Jean-François when he began working on the hieroglyphs in 1820. He also provided documents and books that were useful for his research.

In conclusion, without the help of his brother Jacques-Joseph, Jean-François Champollion may not have deciphered the Egyptian hieroglyphs. Jacques-Joseph's efforts were decisive for his brother's career and research, which ultimately succeeded in deciphering the Rosetta Stone in 1822.
