### The four risks of AI for students (and how to avoid them)


Title: The Four Potential Risks of AI for Students and How to Avoid Them

The increasing integration of Artificial Intelligence (AI) into our societies has significant implications for students. While AI can offer many advantages, it can also pose certain risks. Here are four of them:

1. Excessive Technological Dependence: Excessive use of AI can lead to excessive technological dependence in students, which can affect their ability to work independently and develop their cognitive skills. To avoid this, it is important that students master the use of AI and strive not to rely on it completely.
2. Loss of Skills: Excessive use of AI can also lead to the loss of skills in students, as they may become too reliant on AI to solve problems rather than learning to solve them themselves. To avoid this, it is important that students use AI as an additional tool and not as a replacement for their own work.
3. Degradation of the Quality of Education: Excessive use of AI can also lead to a degradation of the quality of education, as students may be exposed to inaccurate or incomplete information. To avoid this, it is important that students verify the sources of information they receive and strive to use reliable tools.
4. Privacy Violations: The use of AI can also lead to privacy violations, as the personal data of students may be collected and used without their consent. To avoid this, it is important that students are aware of the privacy policies of the tools they use and strive to protect their personal data.

In conclusion, AI can pose certain risks to students, but these risks can be avoided by mastering the use of AI, using reliable tools, and protecting their personal data.
