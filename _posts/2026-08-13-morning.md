### 'We are already in a state of scarcity': is the drought at risk of depriving part of France of access to drinking water?


The current drought in France could lead to water shortages in some regions of the country. According to weather forecasts, this situation could persist for several months. Local authorities have already implemented measures to restrict water use, including banning certain types of irrigation and limiting water use in public parks. In addition, some water treatment plants have been affected by the drought, which has led to problems with the quality of drinking water. Authorities have also called for a reduction in water consumption to prevent stocks from running out. Furthermore, measures to store additional water are planned in some watersheds in order to prepare for a potential more severe shortage. Farmers have also been affected by the drought, with potentially reduced harvests and rising prices for agricultural products.

### 'It was unreal': Solar eclipse awes Europe


The solar eclipse of June 21, 2021, fascinated Europe. This exceptional event was observed across much of the continent, from northern Norway to Greece and Spain. Spectators were able to admire a unique natural phenomenon, where the Moon passed in front of the Sun, creating a lunar shadow on Earth. This total solar eclipse was visible in a narrow band that crossed northwestern Europe from southwest to northeast. Observers noted that during the eclipse, the Sun lost almost all of its light and appeared as a reddish disk. This solar eclipse was considered a unique opportunity for professional and amateur astronomers to observe phenomena such as the solar corona, which is only visible outside the Earth's atmosphere during an eclipse. The solar eclipse was also used for scientific experiments, such as accurately measuring the speed of the Earth's rotation around the Sun. Finally, this solar eclipse generated great public interest and attracted thousands of spectators in the areas where it was visible.
