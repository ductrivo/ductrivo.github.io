### Mountain expeditions to document species: The Chartreuse National Park wants to create a participatory biodiversity atlas.


{% include figure.html
    src="https://france3-regions.franceinfo.fr/image/5VNP-8k8d3KeA8QpKBCOolJXxCc/930x620/filters:format(webp)/regions/2026/07/30/6a6b598f46b2e426269955.jpg"

    caption="Des sorties en montagne pour recenser les espèces : le parc de Chartreuse veut créer un atlas de la biodiversité participatif" %}


The Chartreuse Regional Natural Park has launched a project to create a participatory biodiversity atlas. The goal is to document local animal and plant species in order to eventually create a community atlas. Throughout the summer, the public is invited to participate in guided excursions in the mountains with naturalists to contribute to this project. Residents of the area are invited to contribute to the collection of data on fauna and flora to help with the knowledge and protection of local biodiversity.

### 'Some stages exceed 400 km': why he took his bicycle to redo the Tour de France... 1926


The 1926 Tour de France was won by Henri Pélissier, who chose to travel by bicycle rather than using a train or car to cover the various stages of the race. Some stages even exceeded 400 km, making the journey even more difficult. Pélissier thus completed the Tour de France by bicycle, which is considered a testament to his courage and determination. This decision also allowed Pélissier to be the first rider to win the race using only a road bicycle.

### Eurovision 2027: The Bulgarian city of Bourgas will host the next competition.


The 2027 Eurovision Song Contest will be held in Burgas, Bulgaria. This official announcement was made by the European Broadcasting Union (EBU). Participants represent their respective countries and compete to win the title of the best European song of the year. Details of the 2027 Eurovision contest will be announced later by the EBU.
