School start-of-year allowance: key information

The school start-of-year allowance will be paid on Tuesday, August 18, 2026, in the metropolitan area. Here are the details to know in order to receive this financial assistance:

Amounts:
- Students in primary school (CP) to lower secondary school (CM2) receive an allowance of 95 euros.
- Students in CE1 to upper secondary school receive an allowance of 170 euros.
- First-year university students receive an allowance of 380 euros.
- Postgraduate students receive an allowance of 470 euros.

Income conditions:
- For families with an income of less than 1,925 euros per month, the allowance is paid without conditions.
- For families with an income between 1,926 and 3,874 euros per month, an application must be submitted to receive the allowance.
- Families with an income of more than 3,875 euros per month are not eligible for the school start-of-year allowance.

Procedures:
- Families must submit their application before August 14, 2026, to receive the school start-of-year allowance in 2026.
- The application can be submitted on the website of public services or in town halls.
- Families must provide documents such as identity cards, proof of income and school certificates to submit their application.

This epidemic began in August 2018 and quickly spread to several provinces of the country. According to the latest estimates, more than 1,600 cases have been confirmed and approximately 1,000 people have already died. The Congolese health authorities have declared a national health emergency in response to this epidemic, and international aid teams have been dispatched to help control the spread of the virus. The DRC government has also launched a vaccination campaign to protect the most vulnerable populations. However, access to remote rural areas and resistance from local communities make it a significant challenge to control this epidemic.

### Nightmare on an American aircraft carrier: What's happening on the USS Abraham Lincoln?

However, further investigations are still needed to determine the exact cause of the fire.
