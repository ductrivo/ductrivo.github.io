### What you need to know about the MoDem MEP assistant case, which is being appealed


{% include figure.html
    src="https://www.ici.fr/pikapi/images/1aeca9c6-2c12-4558-b321-c25e2bf02ee9/920x517?webp=false"
    alt="What you need to know about the MoDem MEP assistant case, which is being appealed"
    caption="What you need to know about the MoDem MEP assistant case, which is being appealed" %}


The appeal trial of the case involving assistants to French MEPs from the Democratic Movement (MoDem), led by François Bayrou, began this Wednesday before the Paris Court of Appeal. The former Prime Minister and former mayor of Pau had been acquitted in the first instance.

The case concerns allegations of tax fraud related to the use of public funds to finance the parliamentary assistants of the MoDem. The accusations were initially made in 2017, and the trial began before the Paris criminal court in 2019.

In the first instance, François Bayrou was acquitted, but several other members of the party were convicted of aggravated tax fraud and abuse of public funds. The sentences ranged up to 100,000 euros.

The appeal trial began this Wednesday with the testimony of several people involved in the case, including François Bayrou and other members of the MoDem. The lawyers for the parties presented their arguments before the Court of Appeal, which will issue a final judgment on the case.

The stakes of the case are significant for the MoDem, which has been affected by several political scandals in recent years. The party has lost many of its recent elections and has seen its influence significantly diminished. If François Bayrou is convicted on appeal, it could have serious consequences for the future of the MoDem.
