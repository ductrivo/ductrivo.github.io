4. Ethics and bioethics: The creation of organoids raises important ethical questions, particularly regarding the personality and status of these artificial structures. It is necessary to clearly define the rules for their use in medical research.

In conclusion, organoids are a promising tool for understanding and treating neurological diseases, but their use must be regulated and ethical.
