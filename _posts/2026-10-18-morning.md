## Isère


### Demonstration in Grenoble on October 17th: thousands of protesters, 51 arrests, 3 injured, and significant damage.


{% include figure.html
    src="https://www.ici.fr/pikapi/images/0ab19633-5bc1-400a-813d-7c482a914e67/920x517?webp=false"
    alt="Demonstration in Grenoble on October 17th: thousands of protesters, 51 arrests, 3 injured, and significant damage."
    caption="Demonstration in Grenoble on October 17th: thousands of protesters, 51 arrests, 3 injured, and significant damage." %}


On October 17th in Grenoble, thousands of protesters mobilized against rising prices. According to the Isère Prefecture, approximately 13,000 people participated in the marches in the city's streets, while the CGT estimates the number to be 22,000. The demonstrations were marked by arrests (51 according to sources), injuries (3), and significant damage in the city center, including looting and vandalism. The march was primarily organized against the rising cost of living, but this was partly overshadowed by these incidents.
