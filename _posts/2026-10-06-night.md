### North Isère: Micro-daycare centers Callihop placed in receivership


{% include figure.html
    src="https://www.ici.fr/pikapi/images/d0830523-799a-44cc-8c1d-dcf7aab796e4/920x517?webp=false"
    alt="North Isère: Micro-daycare centers Callihop placed in receivership"
    caption="North Isère: Micro-daycare centers Callihop placed in receivership" %}


The Callihop network of micro-daycare centers, located in North Isère, was placed under insolvency proceedings by the Vienne commercial court on October 6th. This measure is taken due to persistent financial difficulties over several months.

The Callihop network has over 20 locations in the Rhône-Alpes region, offering childcare solutions for parents who work or are in training. However, financial problems have led to delays in payments to employees and suppliers.

The decision by the commercial court does not end the case. In fact, another decision must still be made by the Grenoble administrative court, which will determine whether the Callihop micro-daycare centers can continue their operations or not.

This situation puts the future of the 200 employees of the network and more than 1,500 children who are cared for daily at risk. Parents, employees, and local authorities hope that the decision by the Grenoble administrative court will allow the Callihop network to be saved.
