### Cremation in Isère: 'We have now exceeded 60% this year' during funerals.


{% include figure.html
    src="https://www.ici.fr/pikapi/images/d7a56fb1-6e2a-4e3f-8ec5-82f17342ffd7/920x517?webp=false"
    alt="Cremation in Isère: 'We have now exceeded 60% this year' during funerals."
    caption="Cremation in Isère: 'We have now exceeded 60% this year' during funerals." %}


In the Isère department, cremation ceremonies exceeded 60% during funerals that year. A few days before All Saints' Day, ICI Isère invited Laurence Mathon, director of the Intercommunal Funeral Services (PFI) in La Tronche, to discuss the evolution of funeral practices.

Laurence Mathon highlighted that the trend towards cremation has increased in recent years due to growing demand from families and a change in habits and preferences. She also mentioned that cremation is more environmentally friendly, economical, and allows for the scattering of ashes at a location chosen by the family.

Furthermore, Mathon explained that the PFI have implemented measures to meet the needs of families during the current health crisis, including limiting the number of people present at ceremonies and implementing safety measures.

She also emphasized that the PFI have established a support service for families to provide them with information on funeral practices, as well as emotional support.

In conclusion, the trend towards cremation has increased in recent years in Isère, and the PFI have implemented measures to meet the needs of families during the current health crisis.

### 'We want to change people's minds': In Grenoble, a new space dedicated to natural burials is being created.


{% include figure.html
    src="https://www.ici.fr/pikapi/images/7a673463-a9df-43e4-9078-c928156d7c0f/920x517?webp=false"
    alt="'We want to change people's minds': In Grenoble, a new space dedicated to natural burials is being created."
    caption="'We want to change people's minds': In Grenoble, a new space dedicated to natural burials is being created." %}


In Grenoble, a new space is being created to promote natural burials. In anticipation of All Saints' Day and the Day of the Dead, on November 1st and 2nd, the Saint-Roch cemetery is creating a dedicated area for this practice. This initiative aims to change attitudes towards death and burial.

This area allows for the burial of the deceased without embalming or toxic chemicals, which is more environmentally friendly. The bodies are simply placed in wooden boxes or biodegradable bags. This practice is also more economical as it does not require the purchase of expensive funeral products.

This initiative is a response to the growing demand for this form of burial, which is considered to be more environmentally friendly and closer to nature. This area is accessible to people of all beliefs and convictions.

The goal of this project is to promote a more environmentally friendly form of burial, which would help to reduce greenhouse gas emissions associated with traditional burials. This initiative is also a way to educate the public about more sustainable and environmentally friendly burial options.

This area has been open since October 1st and is accessible to people interested in this form of burial. It offers an alternative to the traditional practice of burials, which can be more expensive and less environmentally friendly.

### Why is there no longer any nuclear research at the CEA in Grenoble?


{% include figure.html
    src="https://www.ici.fr/pikapi/images/9a3d11fe-7659-4fa8-a8bc-cc4bebdbdd94/920x517?webp=false"
    alt="Why is there no longer any nuclear research at the CEA in Grenoble?"
    caption="Why is there no longer any nuclear research at the CEA in Grenoble?" %}


The Grenoble Nuclear Research Center (CEA) ceased its research into civilian applications of the atom in the late 1980s and early 1990s. Initially created for this purpose in the 1950s, the Grenoble CEA had redirected its mission due to a decrease in public funding for this type of research. Today, the center focuses on research and development in the fields of nuclear energy, materials physics, health and the environment, as well as in other technological areas.
