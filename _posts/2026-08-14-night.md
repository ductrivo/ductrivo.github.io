### Fire in the Landes region: What is the purpose of the Chinook CH-47D, this Australian helicopter, which is being used for the first time in France?


Fire in the Landes: What is the Chinook CH-47D, the Australian helicopter used for the first time in France?

The Chinook CH-47D is a military helicopter used for transport and firefighting missions. It was deployed in the French region of Landes to combat the fires that have ravaged the area for several weeks.

This helicopter, owned by the Australian army, is equipped with a firefighting system that allows it to drop large quantities of water on the fires. It can also transport rescue teams and equipment to combat the fires.
