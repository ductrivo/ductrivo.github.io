### Drôme. The large-scale project to preserve this century-old dam is underway under the waters of the Isère.


{% include figure.html
    src="https://cdn-s-www.ledauphine.com/images/8F96A34A-525B-4B4F-A76E-8D1AB02A5597/NW_listB/le-chantier-d-envergure-destine-a-preserver-ce-barrage-centenaire-se-deroule-sous-les-eaux-de-l-isere-1792002592.jpg"
    alt="Drôme. The large-scale project to preserve this century-old dam is underway under the waters of the Isère."
    caption="Drôme. The large-scale project to preserve this century-old dam is underway under the waters of the Isère." %}


The renovation project of the Beaumont-Monteux dam, located in the Drôme department, began under the waters of the Isère River. This major project aims to preserve this century-old dam, built in 1920. The project is scheduled to be completed in March 2027.

The current work involves reinforcing the dam's structure and replacing worn components. The work also includes the installation of new barriers, leak detection systems, and safety equipment.

Significant progress has already been made since the project began in 2019. Workers have replaced the worn components of the dam, including reinforced concrete barriers and leak detection systems. They have also installed safety equipment such as surveillance cameras and alarm systems in case of danger.

There are still some steps to be completed before the project is finished. Workers must complete the installation of the new barriers, reinforce the dam's foundations, and install a flood control system. They must also conduct tests to ensure that the dam is in good condition and can withstand any type of situation.

The renovation project of the Beaumont-Monteux dam is estimated to cost 100 million euros. It is being funded by the State, the Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes region, and the Drôme department. The project aims to ensure the safety of people and property in the event of a flood, while preserving this century-old dam.

2027 Presidential Elections: Trend towards a second round pitting Mélenchon and Le Pen, according to two polls.

According to surveys conducted by the opinion research firms Ifop and Odoxa, Jean-Luc Mélenchon and Marine Le Pen could find themselves facing off in the second round of the 2027 French presidential election.

According to the Ifop survey, conducted among a sample of 1,008 people, Jean-Luc Mélenchon receives 23% of the votes, and Marine Le Pen 22%. Emmanuel Macron, currently in office, would not be eligible for a second term.

The Odoxa survey, conducted among 1,004 people, gives similar results: Jean-Luc Mélenchon receives 23% and Marine Le Pen 22%. The other potential candidates do not exceed 15%.
